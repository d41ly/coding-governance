## 4. Design

### Evidence

- `tools/workflows/review_replay.py:151` is `parse_appendix_rows`, with `CELL_SPLIT` and the `\|` inversion, and it returns only `confirmed` rows and refuses `no-count` and a `liveness` mismatch.
- Report id 30 ran it over 72 records: 14 `CLEAN WITH FIXES` and 42 `BLOCKED` read `no-appendix`, 16 ok, no `liveness` and no `no-count` failure. PINNED at the audit.
- `tools/workflows/tier2-review.js:1626-1628` escapes `|` as `\|`, and `ref` sits left of `verdict`.
- Unit 10's `resolves` key lists the sibling homes a kit's probes name, and `tools/workflows/kit.toml:168` runs this file's `--selftest`.

### Why `read_appendix_table` and not `parse_appendix_rows`

`parse_appendix_rows` drops every row but `confirmed`, and refuses a report with no stated count. The
report's note on ids 4 and 30 rules that such a report takes its rows with no cross-check, and
DEPL-dShippedRelease-118 needs the rows it drops. So the function splits at its one seam, and the
stated-count refusal stays with the replay benchmark that needs it.

- Unit 85 S1's count, and §4's Alternatives line: rows stay the count, and DEPL-dShippedRelease-117 adds the shape line as a guard.
- Unit 85 §10's "no reader of a report's appendix".
- Unit 14 S4's unspecified reader, which unit 85 named: the rows are S3's.
