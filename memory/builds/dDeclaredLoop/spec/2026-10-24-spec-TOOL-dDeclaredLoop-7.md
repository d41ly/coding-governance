## 1. Goal

One engine answers "where is this build, and what next" from witnesses alone: each unit's step, the
build's step, the next item with its actor and its interrupts, items idempotent by witness and keyed
by round or pass and input SHAs, answering from the closed set `act`, `awaiting`, `blocked`,
`terminal`, `stuck`, `invalid-input`, `stale-remote`, and printing its read set and every claim it
used with its key and verdict. `--plan` and `--status` read it, and the `NEXT_RUNGS` ladder, which
reads typed state, is retired (gap-orient-03; the orientation record's `[position]`,
`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-14-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-orientation.md@608549775a18:121`).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The engine, `derive_position(repo, slug, definition=None)` in `tools/loop/position.py`,
  returning a `Position` (§4 Data model). It reads only witnesses, each through the reader the tree
  already holds:
  - commits and their `Pass:` trailers through the memory-tree kit's `commit_trailers.py`, its
    `read_commits` and its `derive_attribution` in the `replace` view (unit 23), imported through
    `tools/loop/loop_lib.py#read_memory_tree` as `tools/loop/loop_transition.py` imports it;
  - the build's recorded moves through `tools/loop/loop_transition.py#read_transitions` (unit 3),
    each with the direction unit 10 derives;
  - the steps, the `[states]` table and the transition rows through
    `tools/loop/loop_table.py#read_transition_rows` over `tools/loop/loop_lib.py#read_definition`
    (units 1, 13 and 15);
  - the items through `tools/loop/loop_items.py#read_composition`, gov's `[[item]]` rows composed
    with the repository's (unit 24), each declared with its actor, needs, outcomes and witness
    (units 9 and 12);
  - the acceptance records through the memory-tree kit's `build_records.py`, its `read_records`
    with `read_acceptance` (unit 17);
  - the run-state file's parked entries and hold facts, `--plan`'s hand-over (S8), and the
    remote-tracking refs.

  `definition`, where given, is a parsed definition read in that call instead of the shipped one and
  the composition, so a fixture plants its rows in memory as `loop_table.py`'s plants are made. No status
  header, README status word or `phase:` fact decides a step. Observed by AC1, AC2, AC9.
- **S2** — A unit's step. The unit states are the `unit` list of `[states]` in
  `tools/loop/loop.toml`, and each is reached on the witness its unit-table row names: `designed` when
  the unit's spec is committed and derives READY, its M2 state read from `--plan`'s hand-over (S8);
  `built` when a pass commit the `replace` view attributes to the unit sits on the run branch's
  `--first-parent` history and a well-formed acceptance record names the unit in `Caused-by` and that
  commit in `Sha`; `tested` when the verdict for the build's prepared merge holds, read until
  dWitnessedBar lands it from the run-state `gates-run` fact naming the bar and the tip it graded;
  `deployed` when the default branch's remote-tracking tip contains the pass commit. A unit reaching
  none of them is `none`. A unit's step tag is the step of the item on the unit table's forward row
  out of its state, `item.design.ready` out of `none` down to `item.deploy.land` out of `tested`,
  found through `read_transition_rows`'s `item-states`, and a `deployed` unit is past its last step.
  The build's step is its least-advanced unit's (FI-1), ties broken by the hand-over's row order.
  Observed by AC1.
- **S3** — The next item: the first item of the build's step, in the composition's order, whose
  witness does not hold and whose needs are witnessed, a `needs` entry that is a list being met by any
  one of its tags, with its actor and the least-advanced unit it serves. The engine reads a witness
  only where a reader exists (§8 F5): a step's exit item on the recorded move out of that step's
  state; the four forward items of the unit table, for each unit, on S2's witnesses; and an item a
  recorded move names as its `item`, at that move's commit. Every other item's witness is unread: it
  prints as a claim whose verdict is `evidence`, and an unread need is unmet. Each item instance
  carries the key `<item tag>@<round|pass>#<inputs>`, `<inputs>` the first eight hex digits of the
  SHA-1 of the blob and commit ids its witness read, sorted and NUL-joined; an instance whose key
  matches a witnessed instance is done and never re-run, and a crashed one re-checks its witness
  before it runs again. Observed by AC2, AC3.
- **S4** — The interrupts, before the next item and in `[position]`'s order:
  - an owner decision the build waits on: an owed parked entry of the run-state file, a kind of the
    driver's `PARK_KINDS_OWED` or a `rescope` act of its `PARK_ACTS_OWED`, with no settling row, or a
    hold whose `hold-code` is set and whose `hold-until` has not passed;
  - mailbox messages, printed `unarmed` until dWellOiledMachine lands the mailbox;
  - witnesses invalidated since the last recorded move, each a claim whose verdict is not `fresh`;
  - dependency gates: a build the README's `parents:` names, read through the memory-tree kit's
    `parse_front_matter` as `tools/loop/loop_parents.py#read_withdrawn_parents` reads it, that has not
    landed (§8 F6: its newest recorded move enters `landed`, or `--plan`'s hand-over gives its derived
    phase as `LANDED`);
  - an undecided ask `--plan` hands over, ranked below the next item (§8 F4).

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- The ladder: `NEXT_RUNGS` and `derive_next_shape`
  (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#derive_next_shape`), read once by `verb_plan`
  (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#verb_plan`). Its inputs are the README roster's typed state, and
  its third rung now takes TOOL-dDerivedDocket-16's undecided ask, `ASK_PLAN_NEXT`.
- `--status` reads the run-state file's typed `phase:` through `read_derived_phase`
  (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#read_derived_phase`) and the first non-terminal unit through
  `nonterminal_units` (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#nonterminal_units`), a grep over status words.
- `plan_state` (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#plan_state`) is the M2 classifier, awk over a
  spec's sections, sliced by a sibling harness; a second implementation in Python would be a second
  answer to one question. `plan_row` prints its rows TAB-separated under `--paths`.
- The `gates-run` fact names the bar and the HEAD it graded (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh@4344df262ed6:2927`).
- The unit table's four forward rows name the four witnesses S2 reads, and `[states]` lists the unit
  states and the build's terminals (`tools/loop/loop.toml@86ccb031eca7:389`).
- A recorded move carries `from`, `to`, `item` and `sha` and no outcome
  (`tools/loop/loop_transition.py#read_transitions`), so `derive_move`
  (`tools/loop/loop_table.py#derive_move`), which needs the outcome and its facts, cannot be replayed
  on one.
- The `[position]` record: only witnesses decide a position; a unit's step is designed, built, tested
  or deployed; the build's step is its least-advanced unit's; interrupts come first in a stated
  order; one call returns the read set and every claim with its key and verdict.
- Items are idempotent by witness: "a witnessed instance is never re-run"
  (`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-15-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-flow-steps.md@d73cb002d028:101`).

### Data model

```python
Position = {
  "slug": str, "state": str,                  # the newest recorded move's `to`, `none` where none is recorded
  "build_step": str,                          # step tag of the least-advanced unit
  "units": [{"id": str, "step": str}],        # none | designed | built | tested | deployed
  "next": {"item": str, "unit": str, "actor": str, "key": str} | None,
  "interrupts": [{"kind": str, "subject": str}],
  "answer": str,                              # one of ANSWERS
  "reason": str,
  "read": [str],                              # "<path>@<blob8>" or "<ref>@<sha8>"
  "claims": [{"name": str, "key": str, "verdict": str}],
}
ANSWERS = ("act", "awaiting", "blocked", "terminal", "stuck", "invalid-input", "stale-remote")
```

### The answer

| Precedence | Answer | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `invalid-input` | the slug names no build; the README or a spec header does not parse; no tracked spec grades as a unit and no roster id is MISSING; a read refuses; or a recorded move no build row holds, as `check_recorded_move` answers |
| 2 | `stale-remote` | the claim on the default branch's remote-tracking tip is not `fresh`: unread, or past its bound |
| 3 | `terminal` | the build's state is in `[states]`'s `terminal` list, or every unit is past its last step and the hand-over names no undecided ask |
| 4 | `awaiting` | an owner-decision interrupt: an owed parked entry or a hold whose `hold-until` has not passed |
| 5 | `blocked` | a dependency gate: a `parents:` build not landed |
| 6 | `act` | a next item exists |
| 7 | `awaiting` | the hand-over names an undecided ask (§8 F4) |
| 8 | `stuck` | none of the above: no item of the current step can run and its exit item is unwitnessed |

`stuck` is the only answer reached by elimination, and its reason names the step and the unwitnessed
exit item. `check_recorded_move(move, tables)` joins `tools/loop/loop_table.py`: a move is held where
a `build` row names its `item`, its expanded `from` holds the move's `from`, and its `to` is the

### The `next:` line

`--plan` keeps its five shapes; `render_next_line` picks one from the engine's answer, and
`NEXT_SHAPES` in `position.py` holds their words.

| Engine answer | `next:` shape, as printed today |
|---|---|
| `act`, its least-advanced unit `none` or `designed` and its spec tracked | the live-unit shape for `<id>`: `<id> (READY - build it)`, or `<id> (<state>)` for THIN or FORKED |
| `act`, its least-advanced unit `none` and its spec missing | `<id> (MISSING - spec it first)` |
| `act`, its least-advanced unit `built` or `tested`: the build waits on its bar | the `terminal` row's shape, `none - every tracked spec is terminal`; where the hand-over names an undecided ask, the ask's shape, as an all-deployed build answers `awaiting` on it (§8 F4, F7) |
| `invalid-input`, no tracked spec grades as a unit | `none - no tracked spec grades as a unit (see the NOT A UNIT rows above)` |
| `terminal` | `none - every tracked spec is terminal` |
| `awaiting` on an undecided ask | `<ask id> (UNDECIDED - plan a unit that closes it, or dispose it)` |

Any other answer prints `none - <answer>: <reason>`, a shape no reader matches as an all-clear, which
is the property the ladder's last rung bought.
