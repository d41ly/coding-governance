## 1. Goal

Every closing-review follow-up under `review.follow-up`'s `while-*` value and every fix pass against one
acceptance red ends at the first round whose blocker and high count does not strictly fall, or at
whichever of `review.max-rounds` and `flow.route-backs` it reaches first. A spec audit under
`review.design.rounds`' `until-*` values ends at its target or its bound, and BUILD-METHOD M4's
spec-audit rule disposes its findings and stops its chain. A follow-up's or a fix pass's open findings
are promoted to units while the build's rework budget remains and to asks after it, never folded into
another round, and a blocking item's open work past the budget ends as `flow.on-exhausted` says.
Today `tools/unattended/unattended.sh#review_state` reads one count, `review_counts` projects the
blockers alone, `verb_review` promotes without asking the budget where, and no verb records a fix pass,
so no rule bounds one (`gap-flow-23`, `gap-flow-24`).

## 4. Design

### The predicate

```
review_state <prior> <count> [<bound>] [<route-backs>] [<target>] [<no-fall>]    round = prior + 1
  (target, default count) == 0                           -> CONVERGED
  no-fall empty, prior non-empty and count >= last prior  -> NON-CONVERGENT
  round > MAX_ROUNDS                                      -> CEILING
  round >= bound                                          -> BOUNDED
  route-backs non-empty and round - 1 > route-backs       -> NON-CONVERGENT     (new)
  otherwise                                               -> CONVERGING
```

The four existing tests and their order are `TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-21`'s (I18). This unit gates the second
on `no-fall` empty and points the first at the target. The route-back test sits last, so a loop whose
round bound comes first exits `BOUNDED` or `CEILING` as before, and one whose `flow.route-backs` comes
first exits `NON-CONVERGENT`, the flow-steps record's "that item's loop ends non-converging". With arguments 4 to
6 empty the function behaves as `TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-21` leaves it. It stays self-contained between its
column-0 `review_state() {` and `}`, reading its arguments and `MAX_ROUNDS` alone, the slice
`tools/workflows/tier2-review.test.sh`'s parity arm cuts and calls with three arguments.

### The targets

| Key value | Word | Target count |
|---|---|---|
| `review.design.rounds` `one`, `fixed-count`, `until-no-blocker`; `review.follow-up` `never` | `blockers` | blockers |
| `review.design.rounds` `until-no-high`; `review.follow-up` `after-high` | `highs` | blockers + round-highs |
| `review.design.rounds` `until-clean`; `review.follow-up` `while-finding` | `all` | blockers + round-highs + round-minors |

`spec-target` is the design value's word and `closing-target` the follow-up value's. `verb_review` maps
a word to its count and holds no value table of its own, so the map lives once, in the module that
derives the bounds (§8 F5). `load_review_bounds` sets `SPEC_TARGET`, `CLOSING_TARGET` and `ROUTE_BACKS`,
and where the module does not resolve its kit defaults answer `blockers`, `highs` and `3`, the fields
`show` prints under the catalogue's defaults; its parse refuses a line of other than seven fields, and
its NOTE names all seven. Each subject's call:

| Subject | Count | Target | Route-backs | `no-fall` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the build slug | blockers + round-highs | `CLOSING_TARGET`'s count | `ROUTE_BACKS` | empty |
| a spec subject | blockers + round-highs | `SPEC_TARGET`'s count | empty | set |
| a fix pass | reds | reds | `ROUTE_BACKS` | empty |

Under `while-finding` a round standing on minors alone is not converged; its blocker and high count of 0
does not fall below the round before's 0, so the next such round is `NON-CONVERGENT` and its minors are
promoted. A terminal exit other than `CONVERGED` now stands on its target count, which may hold no
blocker, so the comment above `verb_review`'s state gate that says every such exit "stands on at least
one BLOCKER" is re-worded to say it stands on a target count above zero; the refusals keep their text.

### The count

The planted flat follow-up is rounds `(2, 1)` then `(1, 2)`: the blocker-only rule reads `2, 1` and
re-arms, this rule reads `3, 3` and ends. The field is new and distinct from the exit's ` · highs <H>`:
on the closing diff review the exit's `highs` is a SUM over every round (the carrier's "Review rounds,
and the loop that ends itself"), so one field cannot carry both. A row field ` · round-highs` cannot
satisfy a reader's ` · highs [0-9]+` match, since `round-` sits between the separator and `highs`.

### The promotion target

| Loop | `promotion_target(build, item, true)` | Row field |
|---|---|---|
| closing review | `unit` / `on-exhausted` | ` · to units` / ` · to asks` |

### The fix-pass loop

```
<ts> fix-pass · item <unit id> · reason reds <N>[ · <STATE>][ · to <target>]
```

`run_fix_pass` runs `check_slug`, `runmd_of`, `check_run_state`, `refuse_if_terminal` and
`check_verb_flag` for `--unit` and `--reds`, as the other verbs do. Its own refusals are three
`fail 37` sites: a unit that `_ids_of` does not return whole or a reds count outside the canonical
decimal, one site for both; `check_fix_pass_open`, which reads the unit's last `fix-pass` reason
through `review_last_reason` and refuses a terminal token, its one site reached by `run_fix_pass` and,
above the claim write, by `verb_dispatch`; and one relay of `load_review_bounds`' or the `target`
verb's refusal. A unit id that `_ids_of` returns whole spells no newline, separator or bypass flag, so
no field-shape refusal is owed. `review_counts` and `review_last_reason` take a third argument, the row
kind, default `review`, so the fix-pass projection is the same awk parameterised, and a `fix-pass` row
projects its `reds`. An acceptance red that never greens records `reds 1`, then `reds 1`, and the second
pass reads `NON-CONVERGENT`.

`until-no-high` meets the driver's refusal of exit counts on a `CONVERGING` round at the record, a
