## 1. Goal

At every step's exit a command scores whether the step's work should be reviewed, and where the step has
a single producing role, that role then answers one question with the score handed: should this be
reviewed, and why (`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-15-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-flow-steps.md@d73cb002d028:94-97`).
Both land in one `recommendation` record, and `review.recommendation` and `review.recommendation.source`
say whether it binds and where it comes from. Today one recommendation exists, Design's, computed inside
`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#print_spec_audit_line` and printed to the owner. The loop definition
declares the eight recommend and reason items and their record kind, but no kind of that name exists in
the record factory, no verb writes the record and no role reasons it (`gap-flow-12`, AF-1, DA-H2).

## 4. Design

### The record

```
# <anchor id> recommendation <n> — design review
**Serves:** recommendation <anchor id>
- **Stage:** design
- **Score:** review
- **Signals:** 3 units in the generated region, a spec grading FORKED
- **Source:** scored-signals-and-reasoning
- **Binds:** no
- **Reason:** pending
- **Role:** command
- **Caused-by:** <anchor id>
```

A score record carries `Signals`, `none` where no signal holds, its `Reason` `pending` where a reason
decides and `none` where none does, and `Role` `command`. A reason record carries `Score` the role's
verdict, `Reason` the role's text, `Role` the answering role and `Answers` the stem of the score record it
answers, and no `Signals`. `check_recommendation_fields` holds that shape both ways: `Stage`, `Source` and
`Role` one word each; exactly one of `Signals` and `Answers`; `Role` `command` exactly where `Signals`
stands. The door also refuses an `Answers` naming no recommendation record of the build. The anchor is the
lowest-numbered unit id of the generated region, the unit `tools/loop/loop_transition.py#resolve_build_anchor`
reads from the same specs; a build with none yet gets the score line and no record. The factory numbers
each record of the kind in turn, so a lap that re-enters Design keeps both scores, and the newest score
record of a stage and the reason record answering it are the pair that decides.

### The signals

`print_spec_audit_line` counts the region's ids with `tools/unattended/unattended.sh#unit_rows` and
`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#row_ids_of`, and grades each tracked spec with
`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#plan_state`. Those lines become `derive_review_signals`'s `design` arm,
which also reads each row's Tier column, counted from the row's end because a title may hold a `|`. The
function sets globals rather than printing, so no caller wraps it in a command substitution. It reads
`FORK_CUTOFF` as `print_spec_audit_line` does today: `verb_preflight` loads it before its call, and
`run_recommend` runs `load_fork_cutoff || return 1` first, as `verb_plan` does.

### The score

| Stage | Score |
|---|---|
| `plan`, `test`, `deploy` | `unarmed` |
| `design` | `none` under `agent-judgment`; else `review` on any signal, `no-review` on none |
| `build` | `review` on any signal, `no-review` on none, under every source |

`Binds` is `no` under `advice`, `yes` under `binding`, and under `binding-above-tier` `yes` where
`_rs_tier2` is set. The deciding record: under `signals-only`, and for every stage but `design`, the score
record; for `design` under the other two sources, the reason record answering it, where Build and Deploy
have no single producing role and Plan and Test no role in this tree
(`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-15-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-flow-steps.md@d73cb002d028:94-97`).

### The verb

```
--recommend <slug> --stage <s> [--reason <text> --verdict review|no-review]       run_recommend
  check_slug; check_verb_flag --recommend --stage; load_fork_cutoff
  key reads, each its own line: policy.py get review.recommendation
                                policy.py get review.recommendation.source        a refusal -> relay site
  every shape of S4, the source's and the score record's after the reads -> the one fail 37 shape site
  derive_review_signals; score and binds by the table
  anchor = the lowest-numbered region id; none -> print the score line, write nothing, exit 0
  build_records.py new recommendation --build --unit --stage --score --source --binds --role ...  -> relay site
  stage_runmd the record
  print  unattended: recommendation — <stage> · <score> · <signals|none> · source <v> · binds <yes|no> · <record>
  print, where a reason decides: the spawn and the reason form, S5
  print, where the record decides, reads review and binds:
         unattended: recommendation binds — unattended.sh --trigger <slug> --subject <s> --cause recommendation
```

`<s>` is `<slug>-specs` for `design`, the spec set's subject, and `<slug>` for `build`. The verb needs no
run-state file, as `--record-round` needs none, and writes no row. It finds `policy.py` and
`build_records.py` with `tools/unattended/lib-unattended.sh#resolve_kit_dir`, as
`read_spec_audit_switch` finds the first and `resolve_index_generator` finds the memory-tree kit; it reads
each key as `read_spec_audit_switch` does, which the policy kit's reader coverage greps
(`tools/policy/check_policy.py#READER_SHAPES`), and calls the factory under `run_bounded`.

### The reason

The main loop scores Design once the spec set is committed, spawns `gov-spec-writer` through
`handoff.py render`, its goal the one question and its items the score line, its return `review` or
`no-review` and a why, and records the answer with `--recommend <slug> --stage design --reason <why>
--verdict <answer>`. The answer is recorded, never acted on: the audit's opt-in is the owner's
(`specAudit`), so no answer opts it in or out.
