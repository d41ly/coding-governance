### The held step (S13, S14)

`derive_phase_position(phase, facts, phase_map=None, extra=None, tree=".")` is the signature
`TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-70` S3 lands at order 36. After the row is chosen, a step of `held-in` is answered

other than `HELD`, else `none`; the state is the row's. The `phase` verb passes the fact as any other,
`--fact held-from=BUILDING`. The qualified `HELD` row, `park-for-owner`, keeps no step. The existing
selftest cases `phase parked` and `phase one qualifier` pass no `held-from` and still read `none`; two
cases join: `phase held-in`, `--fact held-from=BUILDING` reading `step step.build`, and the plant
`held-in-elsewhere`, a `held-in` step on the `BUILDING` row, refused naming the row. `help/loop.md`'s
phase-map paragraph and the module docstring say that a held run reads the step it was held from.

### The phase join (S23)

`check_phase_rows(phase_map, core, states, terminal, extra=())` refuses a named row whose phase is in
