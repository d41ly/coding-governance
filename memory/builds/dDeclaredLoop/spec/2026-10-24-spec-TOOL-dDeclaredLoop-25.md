## 1. Goal

A repository may add a stage of its own, tagged `step.adopter.<name>` with its items
`item.adopter.<stage>.<name>`, spliced between two adjacent gov stages by pinned rows of the build's
transition table. Gov's five stages stay whole and in order; the stage row is an overlay row of the
register's stage kind, which gains its factory and gate here; gov's stage tags join dSafePassage's
step gate; a gov stage's rename beside a splice re-points its rows; the repository stage's items and
exit item are witnessed as a gov step's are, and its boundaries are reviewed through
`review.breakpoints` (gap-flow-19; the owner's FA-3, "Repository stages",
`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-19-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-flow-roles-adoption.md@1720bf7555d3:383`
to :388; P20 as amended).

## 2. Scope (IN)

  `overlay_rows.py compose --kind stage`, run as a subprocess through

  AC11.

- **S2** — The expansion, `derive_splice_rows(stage, data)` in `tools/loop/splice.py`. For a stage
  whose `after` is gov step A and whose `before` is gov step B, R is the build row of `data`'s
  `[[transition]]` table whose `from` holds A's `state`, whose `item` is A's `exit` and whose `to` is
  B's `state`. It yields two rows carrying `tools/loop/loop_table.py#ROW_KEYS`: R1 from A's state on
  R's item and outcome to the stage's state, and R2 from the stage's state on the stage's `exit` and
  R's outcome to B's state, each with R's `witness`. The stage's state is derived by
  `derive_stage_state` as `adopter-<name>`, never typed. The composed table holds R1 and R2 and not R.
  Observed by AC1, AC6.

  `tools/loop/loop_parents.py#read_policy_kit`; `records`, the change records
  `tools/memory-tree/build_records.py#read_changes` reads; and `items`, the item kind's composed
  rows. Observed by AC2, AC3, AC4, AC7, AC8.
- **S4** — A gov stage tag succeeded beside a splice: a stage whose `after` or `before` names a tag
  the definition's steps do not hold, which a change record ends with a `Successor`, resolves through
  that successor, record by record, to a step tag the definition holds, before the frame rules read
  it, and its rows derive against it. Each resolution prints
  one line naming the stage, the old tag and the new. A successor of `none`, or of more than one
  tag, is refused naming the record. Observed by AC6.
- **S5** — The stage's items are the item kind's rows: overlay `[[item]]` tables tagged

  `step.adopter.<stage>` is in `stages` and its `step` is that tag, and returning that stage's program
  beside the five. `tools/loop/loop_items.py#run_check` composes the stage kind after the item kind
  answers and passes the composed tags; a stage composition answering unarmed admits no stage, so such

### The expansion

```
R  split into  R1: from [state(A)], item exit(A), outcome R.outcome, to adopter-<name>, witness R.witness
               R2: from [adopter-<name>], item stage.exit, outcome R.outcome, to state(B), witness R.witness
```

Every other row of gov's table stays as shipped; a backward row into A or B is untouched, so a route
back from B to an earlier gov stage passes over the repository stage, and a build passes it whenever
it passes its two neighbours.

### The frame rules

| Rule, in `check_splices`' order | A refusal names | Plant |
|---|---|---|
| the tag is `step.adopter.<name>`, one name segment, no gov step tag's last segment | the tag, `adopter` | `replaces` |
| no gov step tag is a member of `exclude` | the gov stage taken out | `removes` |
| `after` and `before` are gov step tags, B's `order` A's plus one | the missing or non-adjacent neighbour | `reorders`, `before-plan`, `after-deploy`, `non-adjacent` |

| `exit` is an item of the stage whose outcomes hold R's outcome routed to `transition` | the exit | `exit-elsewhere` |
| each of the stage's items holds under `check_witness_classes` | the item | `exit-unwitnessed` |
| an `item.adopter.<stage>.<name>` no stage row declares, refused by `check_program` | the item | `orphan-item` |
