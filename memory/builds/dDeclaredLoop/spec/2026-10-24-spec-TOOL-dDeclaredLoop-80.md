derive_conf_reads(path):                              # 76 S2's, one set added
    tree = ast.parse(read(path), path)

`ast.walk` over a definition reaches its decorators, its arguments, its return annotation and its
body. `read_planted_root`, `transplant` and `fixtures` hold neither token, so a reader named that way
stays in the population; the match is on whole `_`-separated tokens, and case-sensitive.
