# M4 spec audit — the aTetheredConvoy spec set, all seven units

**Serves:** spec-audit DEPL-aTetheredConvoy-1..7  <!-- inferred: its H1 names the spec set, all seven units -->

Five primed lenses over the whole set (underspecification · contradiction across the build method's
four agreement axes · unstated assumption verified against source · prior art · liveness), then
batched skeptics defaulting to REFUTE inside the review protocol's hard cap, then one synthesis.
Sixty candidate findings, eleven refuted, forty-nine surviving, nineteen of them blockers.

Every surviving finding is folded. Each spec carries its fold in a rev bump with a revision-log line
naming what disagreed. Two folds changed a DECISION rather than a wording: unit 1's fork on the
project-owned role is re-resolved, because the audit measured that the option taken would have won
zero destinations; and unit 7's reuse audit is rewritten, because its "no seam exists" claim was
false against a seam already on the merge bar.

## Verdict: BLOCKED

The set is unusually well-grounded work: seven units in a stated order, sixteen forks resolved with reasons, and a large number of measured claims about `tools/govkit/govkit.py`, the seventeen descriptors, `tools/gate-legs.json` and `tools/run-gates.sh` that check out against source. The verify pass refuted eleven candidate findings on exactly those grounds. What survives is not scatter — it clusters into four load-bearing holes, each of which stops a builder cold rather than degrading the result: unit 1's resolver has two reduction keys and its flagship criterion asserts a role the stated key cannot produce (and, after unit 1's own S3, a role no descriptor in the tree wins at all); unit 3's per-file claim quantifies over five `kind = "flat"` entries whose `home` is `tools`, turning a stated one-file exposure into ~626 reds, and its no-repairs-beyond-S7 clause collides with three separate populations S7 does not name; unit 4's baseline demands a run-everything escape its own frozen `[gate_runner]` vocabulary has no key for, and the same escape makes AC3's liveness fixture unreachable; and the nine-step hard-order tuple that units 1, 4, 5 and 6 all key on is enumerated nowhere, with the stated 3+6 arithmetic leaving units 5 and 6 no slot for steps they demonstrably implement. Units 2, 6 and 7 carry real but narrower defects. A builder cannot start on unit 1, 3 or 4 as written; unit 7's central mechanism (the branch anchor and the enumerator's matcher) has no shape at all. Fold the edits below and the set is buildable — most of them are single-sentence scoping statements, not redesigns.
