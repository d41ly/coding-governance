- **Defect:** `verb_abort` writes `witness` = HEAD of the tree that runs `--abort` and then commits the record (`unattended.sh@0c16a66b:4746-4752`), so the commit carrying `phase: ABORTED` descends from its witness. Any ABORTED record read from the tip therefore has its witness on the tip. All 8 gov records satisfy this, and 12 of 12 across the corpus were checked. The only OFF readings came from two LANDING stamps (nc `a7e0eb03`, inCMS `eafbff4f4`) that no ABORTED record carries. Two further shapes make it worse. A witness equal to BASE (an abort before the first commit) is an ancestor of every later tip. A witness from another tree is foreign work: dTieredTribunal's witness `ee0e7547` is aBoundedCeiling's commit. The aMeteredTurnstile row is also wrong. Its work was on main through `3214f393` before the abort, and `6e73562c` changed only `RUN.md`.

- **Fix:** ids 1 and 36 carry skeptic-REJECTED finder fixes, and the corrected fixes are written here; the fix for id 29 was judged SOUND.
  - (id 29, sound) Restate the §2 test as what it showed: a record on the tip implies its witness is on the tip. Then either name a record-population negative or say that none exists. Make the asks' negative arm a fixture record whose witness is off the tip, not a free-standing sha.
  - (id 36, skeptic-corrected) Drop the claim that ancestry discriminates, and restate the census headline and the aMeteredTurnstile row. If `--settle` or the signal must judge "work landed", base it on CONTENT rather than ancestry. One option: the run's own diff `base..witness`, restricted to its declared write paths, is still present at the tip and was not reverted. Otherwise call the predicate what it is, "record reached the default branch". The negative arm is a fixture whose work was merged and then reverted, or carried by a merge whose resulting tree drops the run's paths. Ancestry reads that fixture ON, and the content predicate must read it OFF.
  - (id 1, skeptic-corrected) Require that the witness is not an ancestor of `base`, and that `base..witness` contains a commit attributable to the run: its slug in the subject, or a commit touching its own build folder. Refuse, numbered, when this cannot be determined. Add liveness arms to ask 5 for a witness equal to base and for a foreign-tree witness (the dTieredTribunal shape). Both must read OFF. Do not depend on branch-ref reachability.
