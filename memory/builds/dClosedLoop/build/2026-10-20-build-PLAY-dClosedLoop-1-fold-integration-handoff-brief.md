# Fold integration handoff brief: the whole-design audit's last fold writer

**Serves:** research PLAY-dClosedLoop-1

The integration writer's handoff renders its goal, items, acceptance, gates and non-goals from this brief record,
through `handoff.py render --input prompt=<this file>`. Eight fold scripts and the mint step fold the audit's findings
and the owner's answers. This pass folds what they leave: two later answers, the rows the widened reconciliation probe
needs, and the edits two writers asked of each other.

## The prompt

Fold the fold's remaining items, as one fold script that runs after the eight.

## The brief

### Goal

Fold every item your brief (`input:spec`) lists into dClosedLoop's design records as the eight fold scripts leave them,
so that the build's ten final checks pass after your script and the mint step. Write every edit into one fold script in
your scratch folder, and bring any real design choice to the owner as a fork, unfolded.

### Items

Each item your brief lists, in its order.

### Acceptance

- Every item has exactly one disposition (fold, duplicate or fork), with its evidence from the folded records.
- The fold script applies after `fold-1.py` to `fold-8.py`: each `sub` and `insert_before` matches exactly once in the
  text those scripts leave, before the mint step.
- The dry run of all nine scripts and the mint step ends `ALL rc 0`, or the return names each red and why.
- Each new clause ends with a placeholder id of your writer number, and no edit moves `[clause-ids]`' next id.

### Gates

None of the bar's legs. Run the build's dry run on your own folder, as your brief says, and report its verdict.

### Non-goals

- No product code: nothing under `tools/`, `skills/`, `coding-governance-agents.template.md` or `WIRE-INTO-PROJECT.md`,
  and no `AGENTS.md` re-render.
- No unit specs for the chain builds, and no status change to an existing build or ask.
- No edit to the audit's own records: its reports, returns, briefs, the round record and the fold inventory.
- No edit to the eight fold scripts or the build's committed scripts, and nothing read from, or written to, the sealed
  copy.
