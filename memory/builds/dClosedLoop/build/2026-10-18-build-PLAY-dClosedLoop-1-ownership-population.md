**Serves:** research PLAY-dClosedLoop-1

In round 2 three verifiers each derived an owner for every node from a worktree pinned at `a169b3350`
(`build/2026-10-17-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-ownership-verify.md` `[task-2]`). Besides the nodes the approved

## [task] The task the skeptic is handed

**Your findings** are the 30 rows of `[findings]` below. Each says: the approved table lacks this node, it is
a distinct node of its kind, and its owner is the proposed one. Try hard to refute each, on any of three
grounds:
1. **Membership.** It is not a node of its kind by the membership rule below.
2. **Identity.** It is a node the table already holds, or another candidate in `[findings]`, under a second
   name. Name the one it duplicates.
3. **Owner.** By the ownership rule below, its owner is another build.

**The membership rule the verifiers applied:**
- Items: every row of every table whose header begins `| Item | Actor |` in the design records, which covers
  flow-steps' step sections and layer-expansions' expansion programs both. A row that names two items names
  two nodes.
- Record kinds: every noun that layer-memory's `[contract]`, a row of v2's `[chain]`, or a "Proven by" or
  "Witness" cell in flow-steps or layer-expansions names as a record kind, or as a record a step writes.

**What the table already holds.** Its record kinds are the rows of the kind table in layer-memory's
`[contract]` (`2026-10-15-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-layer-memory.md`, the table headed
`| Kind | Owner | Its writer, or what names it |`). Its items are the rows of flow-steps' item tables, each
with an Owner column.

**The ownership rule.** A node's owner is the one chain build that implements what the node names or
switches; a build that only calls, reads or runs it is not its owner. For an item, that is the build that
builds the item's program step. For a record kind, it is the build that registers the kind, with its schema
and its writer, through its layer's contract. dTotalRecall builds the registry and the `record` command, and
that alone does not make it every kind's owner.

**Read the records as they are now,** in the working tree under `memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/`. The
verifiers read them at `a169b3350`, so their line numbers may have moved. Two edits have landed since: the
re-cut (`2026-10-18-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-recut.md` `[applied]` and `[reconciled]`), and the order review's
fold (`[order-review]`), which added a sixteenth build, dGroundRules. v2 is
`2026-10-14-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-architecture-v2.md`, and its `[chain]` table's planned-units cells say what
each build builds.

**Return** one table, one row per finding: the finding's id; its node; your verdict; the owner you would give
it; and the reason you observed, as a file and line with the deciding words quoted. A verdict is one of:
- confirmed, when membership, identity and the proposed owner all hold;
- refuted: not a node;
- refuted: duplicates `<node>`, naming the node it repeats;
- refuted: owner `<build>`, when it is a distinct node and another build owns it;
- unverified, saying what would settle it.

For a row of several items, give one verdict for the row, and name any item that differs. Then one line of
counts. Write nothing in the repository, and keep any scratch file in a folder of your own inside the
session scratchpad, never at its root.
