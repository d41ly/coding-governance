# Fold Stage 0 handoff brief: Stage 0 again, its leads fixed or classed

**Serves:** research PLAY-dClosedLoop-1

The Stage 0 pass's writer renders its goal, items, acceptance, gates and non-goals from this brief record, through
`handoff.py render --input prompt=<this file>`. Plan step 3 ends with "Stage 0 again". On the tree that the audit's
fold and its closing pair's fold leave, Stage 0 still finds leads in its K2, K3 and K4 arms. This pass fixes each lead
in the design or classes it from its arm's closed set, so that Stage 0 is green.

## The prompt

Fix or class every lead Stage 0 reports on the folded tree, as one fold script and one classes file.

## The brief

### Goal

Give every finding Stage 0 reports on the tree one disposition. Fix it in dClosedLoop's design records where the
records already settle the answer. Class it, with a class from its arm's closed set and a reason the records support,
where the design keeps it by intent. Bring it to the owner as a fork where closing it needs a choice the owner has not
made. Write the fixes as one fold script and the classes as one file, both in your scratch folder.

### Items

Each finding Stage 0 prints for arms K2, K3 and K4 on the tree, as your brief (`input:spec`) says how to list them.

### Acceptance

- Every finding has exactly one disposition (fold, class, fork), with its evidence from the records.
- The fold script applies to the tree at the commit your brief names: each `sub` and `insert_before` matches once.
- Each class is in its arm's closed set, and its reason is a fact the records state.
- Stage 0 on your dry copy reports findings 0, or the return names each finding left and why.

### Gates

None of the bar's legs. Run the build's dry run and Stage 0 on your own folder, as your brief says, and report both.

### Non-goals

- No product code: nothing under `tools/`, `skills/`, `coding-governance-agents.template.md` or `WIRE-INTO-PROJECT.md`,
  and no `AGENTS.md` re-render.
- No unit specs for the chain builds, and no status change to an existing build or ask.
- No edit to the audit's own records (its reports, returns, briefs, the comparison records, the round record and the
  fold inventory) or to the build's scripts, which are the main loop's.
- Nothing read from, or written to, the sealed copy.
