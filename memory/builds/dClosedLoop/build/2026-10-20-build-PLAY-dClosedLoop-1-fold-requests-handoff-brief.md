# Fold requests handoff brief: the whole-design audit's consolidation writer

**Serves:** research PLAY-dClosedLoop-1

The consolidation writer's handoff renders its goal, items, acceptance, gates and non-goals from this brief record,
through `handoff.py render --input prompt=<this file>`. Five fold writers folded the audit's places into disjoint
records and listed the edits other records need as requests; this pass folds those requests, and classes the
reconciliation probe's findings that their folds raised, as one script that runs after theirs.

## The prompt

Fold the five fold writers' requests and class the reconciliation probe's new findings, as one fold script.

## The brief

### Goal

Fold every request the five fold writers listed, and resolve every finding the reconciliation probe reports after
their scripts, into dClosedLoop's design records as the five scripts leave them. Write every edit into one fold
script in your scratch folder that runs after theirs, merge requests that touch a text another writer already folded
into one text, and bring every real design choice to the owner as a fork, unfolded.

### Items

Each request and each probe finding your brief (`input:spec`) lists, in its order.

### Acceptance

- Every request and every probe finding has exactly one disposition (fold, duplicate, fork or main loop's), with its
  evidence from the folded records.
- The fold script applies after `fold-1.py` to `fold-5.py`: each `sub` and `insert_before` matches exactly once in
  the text those scripts leave.
- Each minted clause takes the next free id in order, and `[clause-ids]`' next id moves past the last; no renumber.
- Each fork names its question, its options and a recommendation, and the request or finding it rests on.

### Gates

None of the bar's legs. You may run the build's dry run on your own folder, as your brief says, and report its
verdict.

### Non-goals

- No product code: nothing under `tools/`, `skills/`, `coding-governance-agents.template.md` or `WIRE-INTO-PROJECT.md`,
  and no `AGENTS.md` re-render.
- No unit specs for the chain builds, and no status change to an existing build or ask.
- No edit to the audit's own records: its reports, returns, briefs, the round record and the fold inventory.
- No edit to the five writers' scripts, and nothing read from, or written to, the sealed copy.
