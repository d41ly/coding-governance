### M1 — The FK-5-3 record door refuses the close path's own writes (ids 3, 6)

- **Where:** architecture-v2.md:1756 (cl-dClosedLoop-0505), against flow-steps.md:438 (`deploy.unlease`), :676-679 (`life.quiesce`, `life.unlease`), :80-82 (closing a mailbox) and :421 (`life.release`), coordination.md:102-104 and :122, cl-0415, cl-0281 and cl-0508.
- **What is wrong:** cl-0505, new in this range, refuses any mail, lease or tombstone write whose ref path or sender names a session other than the writer's own. Its one carve-out admits a terminal tombstone only from the lease's holder or the take-over verb. Two kinds of close-path write fall outside that carve-out. First, after a withdraw, `life.quiesce` can end on `runtime.act-ceiling` with a unit lease still held by an act on another node. `life.unlease` or `deploy.unlease` then writes a terminal tombstone on a lease it does not hold. Second, mailboxes are keyed by sender (`refs/gov/mail/<slug>/<fleet tag>/<session>`), so a receiving build's mailbox holds refs that name other sessions. cl-0415, cl-0281, the mailbox close, `life.release`, Maintain's prune and the take-over in cl-0508 all prune those refs.
- **Consequence:** a withdraw or close stalls, or a builder adds a bypass the clause never states. Widening the door to admit deletes would reopen the cross-session forgery that FK-5-3 closes. Two records now give different answers to who may write a terminal tombstone. The clash shows only when the clauses are read together, and no check joins them.
- **Fix:** the skeptic judged id 3's fix sound and rejected id 6's, so the fix below is id 3's together with the skeptic's correction for id 6.
  - In cl-0505, name as admitted writers the unlease commands, which run under the build lease, and the supervisor's prune, alongside the holder and the take-over verb.
  - Admit a delete of a ref under the build's own `refs/gov/mail/<slug>/` only from that build's closing verbs (`deploy.unlease`, `life.release`, and the withdraw or supersede of cl-0281), and only after the copy into the build folder. Each such verb must act under the build's lease, as its holder or through the take-over verb, or be the owner's guarded command.
  - Add three checks. A planted closing build that holds a cross-session mail ref and is closed by each verb must be admitted. A prune by a session holding no lease must be refused, and so must a delete of another build's mailbox. Add a green case for a withdraw that unleases a unit held on another node.
- **Grade:** id 6 was found high. The skeptic re-graded it medium because it is a contradiction in design text that the spec writer meets while reading the clauses together, and it ships nothing.
- **Left-shift gate:** a probe that joins each ref-write clause, on mail, lease and tombstone refs, to the writers that every other clause names for that ref, and reds a named writer the door refuses.
