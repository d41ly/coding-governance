### M2. The queue's start condition leaves out outstanding reservations (id 2)

- **Where:** v2:1511.
- **What:** The ledger admits a call "while the agents in flight and the reservations stay under" `runtime.agents-in-flight`. The new queue starts a call "when the agents in flight fall under runtime.agents-in-flight with room for its declared fan" and does not mention reservations.

Add a fixture with two fan-4 Workflow calls under a limit of 6, submitted before the first SubagentStart, where the second waits until the first's slots free.
