### H1 — `build.unit-lease` waits on every earlier unit's whole tail, so units run one at a time and Stage 0 reports a cycle (21)
- **Where:** memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-15-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-flow-steps.md:281
- **What is wrong:** The B2-F8 fold gives `build.unit-lease` the Needs "`build.base`, and `build.reuse-check` of each unit the declared order puts before it". At the base, the Needs was `build.base` alone. `build.reuse-check` needs `build.scope`, which needs `build.acceptance`, which needs `build.unit-pass`, which needs `build.unit-lease`. So unit N cannot be leased until unit N-1 has been built, accepted, scoped and reuse-checked. The next row (`build.unit-pass`, :282) says "disjoint write sets in parallel", and `design.streams` (:235) groups units that several node units can take at once.
- **Consequence:** The Build step's graph runs units serially while its prose promises parallel builds, and dScopedBuild's specs will inherit one reading or the other. Stage 0's K1 at the head prints `build.unit-lease: a cycle among Needs: build.unit-lease > build.reuse-check > build.scope > build.acceptance > build.unit-pass > build.unit-lease`, which it did not print at 10c0c4fb4.
- **Fix (the finder's fix was judged UNSOUND, so this is the skeptic's corrected fix):** Make the lease's Needs `build.base` alone, kept one per unit. Move the ordering into the Does/Then cells: a unit's lease is taken only once every unit its spec declares as a dependency has its `build.reuse-check` witnessed clean, with that dependency record as the witness. Disjoint units then stay parallel, and no item-level Need edge leads back into the unit's own pass chain. Re-run stage0 K1 to confirm the cycle is gone.
- **Twin:** M1 (finding 3) is the same defect at medium.
- **Left-shift gate:** Make Stage 0 K1 a required check on any fold that edits flow-steps Needs cells. Also add a lint that reds a Needs cell which quantifies over other instances of its own item ("each unit ... before it").

### H2 — A patch draw on a closed root has no defined path once the root's terminal tombstone is pruned (22)
- **Where:** memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-14-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-architecture-v2.md:1761 (cl-0440), with cl-0134 at :1749, the `leases.queued-claim-days` row at :1142, and layer-runtime:465.

(b) State the draw's path after the prune, for example re-creating the tombstone at its recorded last epoch plus one through a compare-and-swap on the transition record. Add a planted draw on a root whose tombstone has been pruned to cl-0440's witness.
- **Twin:** M2 (finding 8) is the same defect at medium.
- **Left-shift gate:** Add a bug-class checklist entry: a clause that acquires a record another clause garbage-collects must state the post-collection case and plant it.
