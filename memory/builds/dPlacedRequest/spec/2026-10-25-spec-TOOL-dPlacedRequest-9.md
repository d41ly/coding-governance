## 1. Goal

The kickoff manifest has no record kind and no instruction unit, and the card reads a guide
(gap-orient-20, `memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-14-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-gap-register.md:151`).
The memory plan's `[pockets]` row places it as "node: units at level 2 that the card reads", keyed
by tag (`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-18-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-memory-plan.md:72`). This
unit moves the manifest's §B orientation facts into level-2 instruction units of the repository's
overlay, through an upgrade step whose down restores them, and renders those units into a
`## pockets` section of the orientation card, so the facts live once, as units, and the card reads
them.

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The fact units. Each top-level `- ` bullet of a kickoff manifest's `## §B — Orientation`
  section, the run before its first `###` sub-head, becomes one `[[unit]]` row of the overlay
  `.governance/instructions.toml` (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32 S1): `tag = "repo.kickoff.f<nn>"`, `<nn>`
  the bullet's two-digit position at conversion, minted once and never renumbered; `level = 2`;
  `type = "constraint"`; `scope = ["reader.card"]`; `trigger = "always"`; `budget` `line` for a text
  of at most 200 bytes and `block` otherwise; `text` the bullet without its `- `; and
  `enforced-by = "none — a fact the kickoff manifest front-loads"`. Observed by AC1.
- **S2** — The selector. `tools/playbook/readers.toml`, the `reader` namespace's declaring source
  (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-10 §4 "The eight"), gains the member `reader.card`, the orientation card as a
  reader of units. Observed by AC2.
- **S3** — The read. A `pockets` verb on `tools/playbook/instruction_units.py`'s command line,
  `python tools/playbook/instruction_units.py pockets [--root <tree>] (--room <bytes> | --all)`, run
  by `run_pockets` beside the `map` verb's `tools/playbook/instruction_units.py#run_map`. It reads the
  units the card's slot composes: `read_units(root)` and `read_overlay(root).units`, through
  `tools/playbook/overlay.py#derive_slot_units` with the selector `reader.card`, its `exclude`,
  `fixed` and `policy_reader` read as `tools/playbook/overlay.py#run_check` reads them. It keeps the
  level-2 units: the selection admits `reader.every` units too, and the level is what keeps the
  charter's every-reader rules off the card. Each unit prints as one whole line, `- ` and the text
  `tools/playbook/instruction_units.py#render_unit` returns, in the composition's order, which for
  the step's rows is tag order, since the overlay writer places rows by level, then tag. The verb
  imports `overlay` inside its body, as `render_unit` imports `policy_value`, since `overlay`
  imports this module.
  With `--room <n>` the output is at most `<n>` bytes: `## pockets`, then the longest run of unit
  lines from the first that fits together with the closing line
  `- … (+<k>) — instruction_units.py pockets --all`, `<k>` the units left out; when every line fits
  there is no closing line. A line is never cut, so no path a unit cites is ever split. When `<n>`
  cannot hold the heading and the closing line for every unit, the verb prints nothing and exits 0.
  `--all` prints the heading and every line, unbounded. With no selected unit it prints the heading
  and `- none declared`, where that fits. A refused overlay read, composition or render exits 1 with
  one line naming it, and a malformed argv exits 2, as `map` does.
  `tools/playbook/overlay.py#read_overlay` reads the overlay at `HEAD`, never the working tree
  (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32 S1), so `pockets` shows a unit only once the commit that wrote its row is
  `HEAD`; a row S5's `up` has written but not committed is not yet printed. Observed by AC3 and AC4.
- **S4** — The card reads it. A new `derive_pockets_block` in `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh`
  reads a composed card on stdin and prints it with its `## pockets` section fitted to the card's one
  cap. It strips any stored section, the run from a `## pockets` line or a `pockets — skipped:` line
  up to the `recent —` line, and measures the rest. It passes the verb `--room` the difference, the
  cap `CARD_CAP_BYTES` minus those bytes, and prints the verb's output immediately before the
  `recent —` line. It resolves the module as an optional sibling
  (`resolve_kit_file playbook instruction_units.py`) and passes `--root "$CARD_TREE"`, as
  `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#derive_map_block` does. It bounds the call as
  `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#derive_overlaps_line` bounds its own: a kill after
  `CARD_POCKETS_BOUND` seconds into a scratch file under the card dir, stdin from `/dev/null`.
  Every failure is one `pockets — skipped: <why>` line in the section's place, its why cut as
  `derive_overlaps_line` cuts its own, printed where it fits the room and omitted where it does not.
  Three callers run it, each before its own cap check, so the section is fitted wherever a card is
  composed: `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#write_card` over `render_card`'s output;
  `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#add_card_body` over the card it composed, so the room is
  what the kickoff's body leaves; and `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#print_rekeyed_card`
  over the re-keyed card. `render_card` itself prints no section. A card is refused only when its
  parts other than the section pass the cap, never for its pockets. The section sits above
  `recent —`, so `skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh#extract_card_parts` files it in the
  startup part, which an append carries and the citation check reads. Observed by AC5 and AC9.
- **S5** — The door. An `[[upgrade]]` row `kickoff-manifest/facts-to-overlay` in
  `tools/govkit/entries/kickoff-manifest.kit.toml` (TOOL-dSafePassage-1 S1), its program
  `tools/govkit/steps/kickoff_facts_to_overlay.py` with `up`, `down` and `witness`, each taking
  `<verb> <target>` as `tools/govkit/steps/adopter_rules_to_overlay.py#main` does. The row's argv
  carries `{gov}` and `{target}` alone: `tools/govkit/upgrade_steps.py#check_argv` refuses every
  placeholder but `{gov}`, `{target}` and `{prefix}`, and `{to}` in a `down` alone, so no argv
  carries the manifest's place. The step reads it itself, the `[answers] manifest_path` value of the
  target's `.governance/deploy.toml`, resolved through
  `tools/govkit/upgrade_steps.py#check_contained`, so a value leaving the target is refused with
  exit 1 and nothing written. With no `deploy.toml`, no `manifest_path` answer, no file at it or no
  `## §B` heading in it, `up` says there is nothing to move and exits 0, writing nothing and no
  before-image, as `tools/govkit/steps/adopter_rules_to_overlay.py#cmd_up` does for a missing key;
  `down` then restores nothing, and `witness` exits 0 naming the absent answer.
  `up` resolves the write command's place through `tools/govkit/upgrade_steps.py#resolve_kit_dir`
  for the playbook kit's `instructions_edit.py`, the target as `here`. A refusal, or a directory
  outside the target, leaves no kit to write or read the units: nothing to move, exit 0, and
  `witness` exits 0 naming it.
  Otherwise `up` builds S1's rows and grades each alone, with `check_statement`
  (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-12 S2) and with `read_overlay(target, candidate=…)` over that row by itself,
  whose `tools/playbook/instruction_units.py#check_unit_row` holds it to the schema and its budget
  (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-20 S1). A bullet either refuses stays in §B as guide text, named on its own
  line by position and finding (§8 F6); a passing one keeps its position as `<nn>`. The candidate of
  every passing row is graded whole, as the adopter-rules step's `cmd_up` grades its own; its
  refusal exits 1, both files byte-identical. `up` writes the before-image through
  `write_before_image`, the overlay text `derive_overlay_text` returns (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32 S8)
  where a row passed, and last the manifest, its top-level §B bullets rewritten to the S6 bullet and
  then the bullets that stayed, so the witness changes last; it exits 0. It knows the S6 bullet by
  every byte up to its semicolon and never converts it. `down` restores both files byte for byte
  from the before-image, each moved bullet back beside the ones that stayed. `witness` holds when §B
  carries the S6 bullet plus only bullets `up` would refuse, and every `repo.kickoff.` row's text was
  a §B bullet.
  Gov runs the step first, on its own tree (P38), after its pass gives a value to gov's two §B
  bullets that carry none, so its run refuses none (PINNED, 2026-11-19 at `eb28fd672`). Having no
  bold label, they are named by opening: "An unattended run is bound by directives"
  (`memory/guides/SESSION-KICKOFF.md@5afa045407:61`) and "An unattended run declares a mode"
  (`memory/guides/SESSION-KICKOFF.md@5afa045407:62`). The template keeps its placeholder bullets,
  which stay as guide text. Observed by AC1, AC6 and AC7.
- **S6** — The manifest after the move. §B opens with one bullet,
  `- The orientation facts are level-2 units the card prints under ## pockets; change one with <path>.`,
  then any bullet that stayed (S5). `<path>` is the target-relative path S5 resolves, so gov's reads

  `skills/session-kickoff/MANIFEST-TEMPLATE.md`, the write-back sentence of "The ratchet — how this

- **S7** — The fixture, `python tools/govkit/steps/kickoff_facts_to_overlay.py --fixture --plant

### Type

Every bullet converts as a `constraint`. The manifest's own rule is "a pointer or a trap, never a
procedure" (`memory/guides/SESSION-KICKOFF.md@5afa045407:47`), and a `pointer` names no whole document
(PLAY-dOrderedCharter-12 S2) while most bullets do; a `constraint` carries a value in its text and
names its enforcer, which `enforced-by` states as none. A bullet either of S5's checks refuses
stays in §B as guide text (§8 F6): the step runs in every adopter's update, and
`tools/govkit/upgrade_steps.py#run_step_walk` stops at an `up` that exits non-zero.

### Migration

The step is the migration: gov's §B moves at the build pass, an adopter's at its next `govkit
update`. Its down restores the manifest and the overlay byte-identical. Any overlay edit stales the
policy mirror `.governance/policy.json`, whose staleness key holds the overlay's blob id, and the
guard refuses a staged mirror while the overlay's staged blob
differs from `HEAD`'s (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32 S7, §5). So gov's pass commits the step's writes
first, then regenerates the mirror by `policy.py mirror --write` in its own commit; on an adopter, the
landing regenerates it, as PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32 §5 states. The step itself never writes the mirror.

### Rollout

Live at landing. A card written before the step ran prints `- none declared` under `## pockets`, and
§B still holds its bullets, so nothing is lost before the move. A card stored before this unit
landed gains its section at its next append or re-key, since both fit it afresh.
