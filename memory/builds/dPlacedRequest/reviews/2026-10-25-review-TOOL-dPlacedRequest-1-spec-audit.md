## HIGH

### H1 — A new holder inherits the previous holder's renewing beat (ids 1, 20)

- **Where:** TOOL-dPlacedRequest-17 §2 S2-S3 and §4 "The age pass" step 2, read with TOOL-dPlacedRequest-1 §2 S8 and TOOL-dPlacedRequest-16 §2 S1 and S4.
- **What is wrong:** under the default `leases.renewal: progress`, unit 1 S8 writes `renews: no` on every write without a witness, including an acquire, a take-over and a draw. Unit 17 ages a lease from the newest `renews: yes` commit on the whole first-parent chain, crossing tenures, and resets nothing at an epoch change. A fresh no-witness create has no renewing beat at all, and S3 gives no verdict for that case.
- **Consequence:** a take-over or hand-over over a chain whose last renewing beat is older than `leases.expiry-hours` reads `stale` the moment it is written, so another node or the driver's preflight can take it at once. Unit 16 AC4 (a second `--for draw` refused `live-holder`) cannot pass on a correct build, and two draws can hold one closed root. A no-witness lease that is abandoned reads undefined, or `unknown`, which refuses every taker for ever.
- **Fix (judged sound for both findings):** stop the renewing-beat walk at the start of the current tenure: the newest commit whose epoch differs from the tip's, or the create. Treat that tenure-start commit (acquire, take, take-over, draw) as renewing under every `leases.renewal` value except `none`, and state the rule in both unit 1 S8 and unit 17 S2. State in unit 17 S3 the verdict for a tenure with no renewing beat. Add a unit 17 AC: a take-over over a chain whose renewing beat was sighted 25 h ago reads `live`, and a second take-over is refused `live-holder`.
- **Gate:** a lease fixture arm that takes over a planted stale chain and asserts `live`, run in the lease leg.
