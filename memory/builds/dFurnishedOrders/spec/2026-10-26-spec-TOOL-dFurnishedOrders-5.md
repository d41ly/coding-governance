## 1. Goal

No role declares what it may act on without re-checking or which inputs its job verifies
(gap-roles-10), and nothing declares what a verifying role receives, so a reviewer's or skeptic's
packet carries whatever its author put in (gap-roles-16). This unit declares each role's verified
input classes in one role field, renders the role's trust profile and context temperature into its
static block as `roles.trust` and `roles.verifier-context` say, and holds `tier2-review`'s verifier
stages to what `roles.verifier-context` allows.

## 2. Scope (IN)

  `tools/roles/role-schema.toml` in TOOL-dBoundRoles-2 S1's grammar, `key = "verifies"`,

## 4. Design

### The values

Item 5 of layer-roles `[trust]` (`layer-roles.md:277-285`) names the verifiers and their subjects:
the reviewer checks the pinned artifact, the skeptic each finding against its cited source, the tester
each red of the verdict.

| Role | `verifies` | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `gov-reviewer` (today `gov-spec-reviewer`, `gov-diff-reviewer`) | `subject` | the pinned artifact through its lens |
| `gov-skeptic` | `claim` | each finding against its cited source |
| `gov-tester` | `verdict` | each red of the verdict it is handed; TOOL-dFurnishedOrders-15 adds `claim` |
| every other role | `none` | a producer acts on what it is handed |

The pass writes the value into every role file present then, TOOL-dBoundRoles-1's new roles among
them.

### The block

`render_trust_block` composes lines from one table in `roles_lib.py`, keyed by value:

| Line | `keyed` | `witnessed-only` |
|---|---|---|
| acts on | `- Acts on: every keyed fact its work order hands it, as given (roles.trust: keyed).` | `- Acts on: witnessed and decided facts as given; an upstream role's claimed fact is re-checked against its source before use (roles.trust: witnessed-only).` |
| verified | `- A claim about code counts as verified when this item read that code, or was handed it with its key.` | the same line |
| claimed | `- A claimed fact guides and decides nothing: a verdict, a position, a landing or a gate rests on a re-derivation.` | the same line |

Then, for a role whose `verifies` is `none`, `- Verifies: none.`; for a verifier,
`- Verifies: <classes> — re-checks its subject, and only its subject.` and one context line:

| `roles.verifier-context` | Line |
|---|---|
| `cold` | `- Context: cold — its packet holds the artifact, the criterion and the claim, and the author's reasoning is withheld.` |
| `warm` | `- Context: warm — its packet may also hold the author's packet and reasoning.` |

```
## Trust

- Acts on: every keyed fact its work order hands it, as given (roles.trust: keyed).
- A claim about code counts as verified when this item read that code, or was handed it with its key.
- A claimed fact guides and decides nothing: a verdict, a position, a landing or a gate rests on a re-derivation.
- Verifies: claim — re-checks its subject, and only its subject.
- Context: cold — its packet holds the artifact, the criterion and the claim, and the author's reasoning is withheld.
```

The lines are the design's own sentences (`layer-roles.md:263-289`, v2:847), so a role reads the rule
and never a summary of it. The block is rendered from fields and keys, so the base-body prohibition arm
of TOOL-dBoundRoles-2 S3 does not grade it.

### The verifier stage

`tier2-review`'s finders and skeptics are its verifier stages; the synthesis judges no subject. Cold
withholds the one carrier of the author's reasoning the brief holds today, the commit-message read in
`renderIntent` for a diff review (`tier2-review.template.js:1114-1128`); a spec audit's brief carries
none, so cold changes no spec-audit prompt. `context`, `specs`, `byDesign` and `priorFindings` stay:
they are the caller's statement of the criterion and the claims, never the author's packet (§8 F2).

- **A trust key per role in the policy file.** `roles.trust` is one value for the repository (v2:1183);

  render-time token is how `{{FANOUT_CAP}}` already reaches it.
