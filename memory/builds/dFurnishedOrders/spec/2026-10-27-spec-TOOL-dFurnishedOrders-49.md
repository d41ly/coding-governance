## 4. Design

### Why this half is disjoint

`TOOL-dFurnishedOrders-48` §4 "Why two units" states the three clauses once. This half writes
`tools/run-gates/selftest-budgets.txt` and `tools/roles/adopt-roles.test.sh`, neither of them a
watched path, and reads the pass commits of units 45 and 46 and the run-state file's rows.

### The budget row, after this unit

```
roles adopter self-test  <ceil(1.5 * (59.737 + S)), at least 60>
    worst of 5 readings 59.737s over every worktree's retained gate-run windows
    (grep -h '^roles adopter self-test' .git/worktrees/*/gate-run/*/*.leg, 2026-10-27), plus S s of sleep
    the arms of units 39, 42 and 46 add, x1.5
```

### The interrupt probe, after this unit

```
patched Popen.wait(self, timeout=None):
    until pidfile exists and is non-empty, at most 15 s: sleep 0.1
    pidfile still absent -> record "DEAD PROBE: stub never started"
    raise KeyboardInterrupt
caller: KeyboardInterrupt reached it, and the pid is gone within 15 s   (unit 46 AC17)
```

### Inventory

Minted: the printed token `DEAD PROBE: stub never started`. No function, flag or file.

### Migration

None: one budget row re-priced and one suite arm, neither stored data.

### Rollout

Live at landing, at order 27, beside unit 48 and after units 45 and 46, whose commits S1 reads.
The roles kit's version moves for the kit epoch leg where its shipped bytes move.

### Files touched (estimate)

- `tools/run-gates/selftest-budgets.txt`
- `tools/roles/adopt-roles.test.sh`

### Alternatives rejected

- **Amend unit 46's Files touched and unit 45's clause 1 now (ids 6, 27).** That is a fold, and this
  disposal folds nothing. The pass commits are also a stronger record than an estimate.
- **Filter the readings by contention (S5).** The budgets file applies no filter, and the report's
  skeptic found no reading that one would admit.
- **A fixed sleep before the interrupt (S6).** A sleep places no signal, and it is the class the
  report's checklist entry names.
