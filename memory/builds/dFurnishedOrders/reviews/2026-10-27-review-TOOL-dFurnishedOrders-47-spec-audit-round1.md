### H2 — Unit 49's interrupt probe never pins whose pid it checks (id 24)

- **Where:** spec-49 section 2 S6; section 4 "The interrupt probe"; section 6 AC5.
- **What is wrong:** S6 and AC5 (and unit 46 AC17) say only "a stub that writes its pid" and "the pid is gone within 15 s". Neither says which pid namespace the stub writes or the check reads.
