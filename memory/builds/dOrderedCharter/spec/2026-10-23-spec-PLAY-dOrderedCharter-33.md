## 1. Goal

A repository's `amends` or `supersedes` edge into a gov unit is pinned to the gov text it was written
against (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32). When gov changes that unit, the edge must neither ride the change
unseen nor flag on a comma: gov's own history shows a whitespace-normalised hash flags no
whitespace-only change, and its noise follows unit size (adopter-instructions, `[history]`). And a
seeded charter template freezes at its install vintage with nothing telling the adopter what moved
(`gap-found-30`). This unit marks each gov change record `editorial` or `semantic`, admits `editorial`
only where the normalised diff is mechanical or the owner ruled it so, lets a pin behind editorial
changes stand, acts on a semantic one as `instructions.stale` says (`order`, `refuse` or `warn`),
follows a rename and orphans an edge on a split or a retirement, joins gov's unit tags to
dSafePassage's step gate with the change record as their one witness, and lists each new gov unit
beside the repository's own (IA-3; v2:1246).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The mark's check. `derive_change_mark(before, after, spelling, ruling)` in
  `tools/playbook/pins.py` returns `editorial` when the two texts, each normalised by
  `derive_pin_hash`'s whitespace rule, are equal (whitespace), are equal once punctuation is stripped
  (punctuation), or are equal once the declared spelling map `tools/playbook/spelling-map.toml` is
  applied to `before` (spelling); or when the record carries an owner's ruling that
  `instructions.edit`'s proof recorded. Anything else is `semantic`. A change record marked
  `editorial` that `derive_change_mark` reads `semantic` is red, naming the record, the unit and the
  first differing word. A change record holds its `Before` and `After` as pins, `<tag>@<hash8>`, not
  as texts, so the check reads both texts from the manifest's blobs at the record's commit and at that
  commit's first parent, and a text whose pin differs from the record's is red as a record that does
  not match its change. Observed by AC1, AC2.
- **S2** — The pin walk. `derive_pin_state(tag, pin, records, units)` follows gov's change records for `tag`
  from the revision whose text hashes to `pin` up to the current text: no record or only `editorial`
  ones → `standing`; any `semantic` one → `stale`; a record whose `successor` is one tag → `renamed`
  to it, the edge re-pointed and its pin kept; a successor list or `none` → `orphaned`. Observed by
  AC3, AC6.
- **S3** — `instructions.stale`, this unit's policy key (values `order`, `refuse`, `warn`, default
  `order`, v2:1246), read by `cmd_update` with `govkit.read_policy_value("instructions.stale", target,
  rev="HEAD")` (TOOL-dSafePassage-9 §4, its `rev` passed to `policy.py get` as `--rev`), the target's
  committed policy at the revision PLAY-dOrderedCharter-46 S1 reads the overlay at, since the choice
  is the adopter's; an unset
  key reads `order` with its reason (TOOL-dGroundRules-3 S2). An exit 2 from the reader, or a value
  outside the three, is a refusal naming the key and carrying the reader's line, which holds the
  playbook kit, as TOOL-dSafePassage-9's mode read holds its row's kit. The mode reaches the drift
  verb (S7) as `--stale <mode>`. Over every `stale` edge at an update:
  - `order`: gov's new text and the repository's unit both render, and one order per edge is filed;
  - `refuse`: the update writes nothing, exits 1 and names each stale edge, until `instructions.edit`
    re-pins it;
  - `warn`: both render, and the edge is printed and journaled with no order.
  Observed by AC4.
- **S4** — Orphans. An orphaned `amends` is held out of the render and an orphaned `supersedes`
  renders on its own as the repository's unit; each files an order under `order` and `warn`, and holds
  the update under `refuse`. Observed by AC6.
- **S5** — The step gate join. A `[[class]]` row `unit` in dSafePassage's class registry
  `tools/govkit/surface-classes.toml` (TOOL-dSafePassage-10): its reader returns every gov unit tag
  from every kit's `instructions.toml` through PLAY-dOrderedCharter-9's `read_units`, its plant takes one out, and its `witness` reads the change
  records' successors, so a gov unit tag can vanish only with a change record naming its successor or
  `none`. The same commit writes the class's `[[class]]` row in `tools/govkit/usage.toml`, since a
  registered class with none is a finding of dSafePassage's docs leg (TOOL-dSafePassage-13 S1, S8): usage
  shape `none`, with a `why`, because a gov unit's usage is no verb, key, hook, leg or page, and the units
  that do carry a `usage` field are PLAY-dOrderedCharter-37's own `unit-usage` class (its §8 F7).
  The two rows land once the tree holds a gov unit row, in a later commit of this unit (the fork
  record's F5). Observed by AC7 and AC11.
- **S6** — New gov units beside the repository's. The update lists each gov unit tag present at the
  target revision and absent at the receipt's, whose `level` and `select` match a level and a
  selection the repository's overlay units hold, one `new-beside` line each, advisory. Observed by AC8.
- **S7** — The update handler. `run_unit_drift(target, gov_root, from_rev, to_rev, stale, write)` is
  `tools/playbook/pins.py`'s `drift` verb, since a kit never imports govkit (TOOL-dGroundRules-1 §4):
  `cmd_update` (`tools/govkit/govkit.py:9144`) runs it as a subprocess after its per-file verdicts and
  before its write loop when the target selects the playbook entry, the kit found through govkit's
  inlined `resolve_kit_dir` (`:196-233`). The verb reads the overlay through `read_overlay`, walks
  every pinned edge, journals `warn` edges under `--write`, and prints JSON: one line per gov unit in
  S22's verdict shape (`stale`, `orphaned`, beside PLAY-dOrderedCharter-32's states), each order as
  data, and whether `refuse` holds the update. `cmd_update` prints the lines among its verdicts, holds
  the update under `refuse`, and writes each order with `write_row_order(outbox, "unit", path, text)`
  (TOOL-dSafePassage-9 S4), the order's `path`, `.governance/instructions.toml#<repository tag>`,
  keying it, as the writer keys its file on `render_order_slug` of that key
  (`tools/govkit/govkit.py:10760`), and `text` the order the verb returned whole, its own title

## 4. Design

### The mechanical classes

`derive_change_mark` normalises each text with the rule `derive_pin_hash` applies (each whitespace run
folded to one space, ends stripped), then tries the classes in order and returns the first that makes
the two texts equal:

| Class | Transform applied to both texts |
|---|---|
| whitespace | none beyond the normalisation |
| punctuation | every character outside letters, digits and spaces removed, runs of spaces folded |
| spelling | `before`'s words replaced through `spelling-map.toml`, then the punctuation transform |

`tools/playbook/spelling-map.toml` holds `[[spelling]]` rows `from` and `to`, whole words only, empty
at landing; a row is gov's owner's to add. A ruling is the change record's `**Ruling:**` field with the
owner's words, admitted only when the record's proof field shows `instructions.edit`'s `tty-cli` or
`signed` proof (`instructions.proof`), PLAY-dOrderedCharter-15's `Proof` field; a ruling on a record whose
`Proof` reads `none — <value>` is red.

Re-read at `f56f6a189`, the change kind's grammar is `tools/memory-tree/build_records.py#KINDS`: required
`Unit`, `Action`, `Before`, `After`, `Mark`, `Proof` and `Caused-by`, `Mark` closed to `editorial` and
`semantic`, and `Successor` on an ending action. `Proof` admits `tty-cli` or `none — <value>`
(`tools/memory-tree/build_records.py#PROOF`), and the command's door writes `none — stand-in door`, so no
`signed` proof exists yet and a ruling is admitted on `tty-cli` alone until one does. `**Ruling:**` is an
optional field the grammar does not list; `tools/memory-tree/build_records.py#read_record` takes an
unlisted field without a fault, so the field needs no change to the kind's required or closed sets.

### The walk and the modes

```
derive_pin_state("practice.verify", "1f2e3d4c", records, units)
  -> standing | stale(records) | renamed(<tag>) | orphaned(<successor>)
```

The revision a pin names is found by hashing each historical text of the unit with `derive_pin_hash`
along the change records; a pin matching none is PLAY-dOrderedCharter-28's red, not this walk's. Each
update line:

```
  stale              [unit         ] practice.verify <- repo.verify-staged (semantic since 1f2e3d4c)
  orphaned           [unit         ] practice.cross-os <- repo.local-os (successor: none)
  new-beside         [unit         ] practice.portable at level 0 beside repo.ships-charter
```

`refuse` holds the update before any write, exits 1, and prints each stale edge with the command that
re-pins it. `warn` appends one tab-separated line per stale edge to
`.governance/journal/stale-edges.tsv` (date, target revision, repository tag, gov tag, pin, current
hash, mode), the journal PLAY-dOrderedCharter-35 also writes for rows. An order is one entry of the
drift verb's output, `{"path": ".governance/instructions.toml#<repository tag>", "body": <body>}`, its
body naming the edge, gov's old and new text, and the command that re-pins or re-points it;
`cmd_update` writes it with `write_row_order(outbox, "unit", row, to_commit, body)` as
`.governance/outbox/update-unit-<slug>.md`.

Re-read at `f56f6a189`, the writer is `tools/govkit/govkit.py#write_row_order(outbox, kind, key, text)`,
four arguments, the file named by `tools/govkit/govkit.py#render_row_order_key` as
`update-<kind>-<slug>`. A row verdict's text comes from `tools/govkit/govkit.py#render_row_order`, whose
title reads "gov no longer ships" for every kind but `render-edited`, a false title for a stale edge. So
the drift verb's `body` is the order's whole text, its own title line, the target revision and the
lines above, and `cmd_update` passes it as `text` with the entry's `path` as `key`; it composes no
second text. The order still lands as `.governance/outbox/update-unit-<slug>.md`.

### The step gate class

```toml
[[class]]
id = "unit"
reader = "{prefix}/playbook/pins.py:read_unit_members"
plant = "{prefix}/playbook/pins.py:write_unit_plant"
summary = "each gov instruction unit, by its tag, from every kit's instructions.toml"
witness = "{prefix}/playbook/pins.py:read_change_successors"
```

```toml
# tools/govkit/usage.toml
[[class]]
class = "unit"
usage = "none"
why = "a gov unit is loaded text, used by no verb, key, hook, leg or page; units carrying a usage field are the unit-usage class"
```

`read_change_successors(root)` returns the tags every change record names with a `successor`, `none`
included, so a retired unit is witnessed by its record and needs no step row. The `usage.toml` row
names no row source, a `none` class having no rows; where TOOL-dSafePassage-13's grammar asks for
`rows` on every class, the builder spells it as that unit's `file` and `installable` rows, its other
`none` classes, spell theirs.

Re-read at `f56f6a189`. `tools/govkit/surface-classes.toml` spells each function as `<path under the
tool root>:<function>` through `{prefix}`, as its `file` and `installable` rows do, and
`tools/govkit/step_gate.py` loads the reader, the plant and the witness from the tip's tree for both
sides of a range, so each takes the tree's root and reads no import-time state. The step gate is
landed (`tools/govkit/step_gate.py`, its keys `tools/govkit/step_gate.py#CLASS_KEYS`). `tools/govkit/usage.toml`
says a `page` or `none` class carries no `rows`, and its `file` and `installable` rows carry none, so the
`unit` row carries `class`, `usage = "none"` and a `why`, and no `rows`. The reader returns unit tags
through `tools/playbook/instruction_units.py#read_units`, which finds nothing until
`PLAY-dOrderedCharter-40` writes the first unit row, at a later order than this unit, and the step gate
refuses, exit 2, a class empty at the tip. So the two rows land in a later commit of this unit, once the

### Alternatives rejected

- **Any change flags.** Over gov's history, 65% of flagged edits change under a tenth of a unit's
  words; flagging punctuation adds orders nobody acts on (adopter-instructions, `[history]`).
- **The writer's own mark, unchecked.** A meaning change marked `editorial` would let a stale pin
  stand, the exact failure the clause plants.
- **A fuzzy text match for a moved unit.** It misplaced or lost 9.9% of edits in gov's history; a
  successor named on the change record is exact.

## 5. Production-readiness checklist

- security: an `editorial` mark suppresses a repository's review of gov's change, so the mark is
  checked mechanically and a ruling needs the owner's proof; `refuse` writes nothing.
- perf / scale: the walk reads each pinned unit's change records once per update.
- error / empty / loading states: no overlay prints nothing; no row kind prints
  `0 row kinds registered`; a pin with no matching revision is PLAY-dOrderedCharter-28's red.
- observability: every pinned edge prints one line, and `warn` journals.
- risks: a punctuation change that alters meaning ("not," to "not") classifies `editorial`; the owner's
  spelling map and ruling are the only widenings, and punctuation is the clause's own class.
- testing: planted releases for every case (AC1-AC9).
- migration: none.
- user docs: `help/overlay.md` gains a section on pins, the three modes and orders.
