### The slot line

A site already prints a skip line; it gains `slot <point> · not-installed: <fallback>` in place of
its free wording, and prints `slot <point> · searched-empty` when the optional kit ran and found
nothing and `slot <point> · filled` when it returned something. A site that printed nothing on
success prints the `filled` line on stderr, so stdout contracts are unchanged.
