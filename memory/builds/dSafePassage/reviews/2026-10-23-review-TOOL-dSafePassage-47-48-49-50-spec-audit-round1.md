### D-5 — TOOL-47's walk has no rule for symlinks, junctions or OS errors under `modules/` (id 22)

- **Where:** TOOL-dSafePassage-47 §2 S3, §4 "The walk" (`spec-47.md:86-94`), §5 error line (`:147-148`).
- **What is wrong:** The walk recurses into every non-candidate subdirectory under `modules/`. A link under `modules/`, or an OSError while listing a directory, is a refusal naming that path. Add a plant `submodule-modules-link-loop` and an AC that it is refused, exits 1 and prints no traceback. Stage its red case with a walk copy that follows links.
- **Left-shift gate:** A checklist entry: every filesystem walk over a tree gov does not own states its link rule and its OS-error rule, with a link-loop plant.
