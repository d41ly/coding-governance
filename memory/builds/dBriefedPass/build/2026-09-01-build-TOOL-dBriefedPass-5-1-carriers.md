## What binds now

makes unit 3's refusal reachable on a sequential pass and not only on a concurrent one. It states the

## The budget nearly refused the whole unit

`memory/guides/UNATTENDED-PROTOCOL.md` had **87 bytes of headroom** against `.memory-tree.conf`'s
`INDEX_CAP_BYTES=61440` when this build opened. Section 12 costs about 1500. Unit 2's verb row had
already spent most of what was left.

Raising the cap is a project declaration and M3 reserves it, so the section was paid for by
compressing **fourteen passages** across sections 1, 2, 3, 4 and 7 — every one a rewording that keeps
each fact and drops words, never a deletion of a claim. The document now sits at **exactly 61440
bytes, zero headroom**. That is not a comfortable place to leave a contract, and it is why the
structural question is PARKED for the owner: raise the cap, split the protocol, or stop giving every
verb a paragraph. The next addition to this file reds the bar before it is written.
