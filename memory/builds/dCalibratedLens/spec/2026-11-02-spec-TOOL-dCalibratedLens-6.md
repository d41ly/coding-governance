## 1. Goal

`memory.item-budget` defaults to `measured`: 96 KB for every role until 30 sliced work orders per role
are measured, then each role's own p90, which the Loop Review derives (v2:372-373, v2:1370; the
owner's MS-2, "Measure, then set"). TOOL-dTotalRecall-9 S6 reads the 96 KB half and hands the p90 to
the Loop Review (its §3), and nothing derives it. This unit derives each role's p90 from the bytes its
work orders' memory slices carry, records it where the budget check reads it under `measured`,
proposes it to the owner through the guarded policy command, and lets the owner's own setting for a
role win.

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- The catalogue row admits `measured`, 96 and 128 only (`tools/policy/catalogue.toml:1457-1461`), its
  `registry` text naming "KB per role" that the `enum` type refuses (`policy.py:154-159`); the `map`
  type's members take a closed list (`policy.py:174-180`), so no row today admits a role's own KB count.
- TOOL-dTotalRecall-9 S6 reads `measured` as 96,000 bytes "until a role has 30 sliced work orders
  measured" and names no reading after; its §3 hands "each role's p90 under `measured`" to the Loop
  Review. Its S8 is the only writer of a work order's slice bytes, the `reads:` list.
- The work order today is the handoff `render_handoff` writes (`tools/roles/handoff.py:312`), its
  marker line carrying the role (`tools/roles/roles_lib.py:79`); TOOL-dTotalRecall-9 S8 ("The work
  order: `render_handoff`") and §10 treat it as the work order.
- The design source: `measured: 96 for every role until 30 sliced work orders per role are measured,
  then each role's own p90` (`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-16-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-systems.md:504`).

### The reading, by example

```
item-budgets — gov-builder · work orders 30 · p90 28000 B · measured
  policy_edit.py propose memory.item-budget."gov-builder" 28
  <the row diff and the command policy_edit.py prints>
item-budgets — gov-spec-writer · work orders 12 · p90 - · 96000 until 30
item-budgets — gov-reviewer · work orders 30 · p90 - · owner-set 64
```

Under `measured`, `check_read_set` then caps `gov-builder` at 28,000 bytes, `gov-spec-writer` at
96,000 and `gov-reviewer` at the owner's 64,000.
