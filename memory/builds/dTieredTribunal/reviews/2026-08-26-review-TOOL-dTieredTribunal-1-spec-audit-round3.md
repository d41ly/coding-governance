**The hypothesis is not supported. The absolute count is identical: 20 fold-created defects from four parallel agents, 20 from one hand.** The share fell 6.5 points only because the denominator grew by three, which is the weaker of the two readings — a share moves when the denominator moves for unrelated reasons, and the count does not. On the measure that does not move for unrelated reasons, one author produced exactly as much new broken prose as four did. Whatever drives FOLD_BROKE in this build, author count is not it.

**The failure mode moved instead, and it got worse.** FOLD_WRONG went from 6 of 29 (21%) to 11 of 32 (34%) — the fold misreading or half-landing an instruction it was given, up by more than half as a share and nearly doubled in absolute terms. **Net-productive on the bounded metric for the second time, and expensive everywhere else for the second time.** The blocker count went to zero, so the fold moved the number the loop is bounded by.
