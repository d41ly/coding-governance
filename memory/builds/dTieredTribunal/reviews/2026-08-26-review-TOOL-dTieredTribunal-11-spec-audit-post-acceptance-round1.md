## The four audit questions, answered

**Is each spec buildable from its own text?** Four of five, yes. Spec-11 is not, on one criterion: AC11 has no reachable verdict, so the unit can never green its own Definition of Done however it is built. Spec-11 also states one deliverable at two sizes (S13 against AC9), which a builder can resolve only by choosing a side.

**Is any criterion still unfalsifiable or self-satisfying?** Three, and all three are fail-OPEN. Spec-13 admits an oversize array literal under the marker with every criterion green (2). Spec-14 AC6 is satisfied by a suite that never gained the arms S5 and S8 promise (5), which is the identical omission that already landed ten missing arms in this same unit. Spec-14's rule 5 is specified against one view and cannot fail on either fixture the spec pins (20). Each of the three passes today and would pass on a build that omitted the thing it exists to observe.

**Does any amendment lower a bar rather than correct a mis-specification?** One does, and it is the one nobody disclosed: spec-14 AC15's rev-4 amendment widens the `check-verifier-fanout.sh` carve-out from the two header lines §3 authorizes to a seven-line edit, by citing a line number inside a block the non-goal never sanctioned (14). Two others are correct in substance and read as lowered bars only because their predecessors were left standing beside them — AC9 at rev-7 (the per-mechanism convention is genuinely what the file uses, but S13 still demands per-site, 12/3/29) and AC11 at rev-6 (zero `tools/` hits was unreachable and the amendment is right, but the exclusion clause it replaced is still in the sentence, 13/1). Correcting an amendment and deleting its predecessor are two edits, and this fold made the first one three times and the second one never.

**Does any revision-log entry claim a repair the spec text does not carry?** Three. Spec-11 rev-7 states the AC9/S13 repair as done and touched only AC9 (29). Spec-11 rev-6 states the AC11 repair as done and left the contradicting clause (13). Spec-14 rev-4 describes a subject/legend generalisation and delivers a widened non-goal (14).

**Blocker first — one clause.** Spec-11 AC11's closing exclusion, narrowed to name the two `tools/` carriers or deleted outright. Nothing else in this record is a precondition for anything.

**Then the four fail-open criteria, because they are the ones a green bar hides.** Spec-13's oversize-literal DENY arm and criterion (2). **Then the three text repairs.** S13's second sentence (12/3/29), the carve-out's line budget and its rev-4 disclosure (14), and the `21` that was never 20's neighbour (19).

**Recorded and not folded.** Precision 0.41 over a twice-folded subject is a tuning datum for the next pass, not a defect in this one.
