## 1. Goal

The lexicon's two gateable drift signals reach the bar through drift-audit's engine, which imports
the optional lexicon kit to compute them, instead of as a leg pack the lexicon registers (gap-lex-08).

through the `pack` key, the parents' spelling of Test's leg-pack point, and leaves drift-audit
importing nothing of the lexicon (lexicon review `[enforcement]` item 6 :351-353, `[vocabulary]`
:414-417).
