## 1. Goal

Measure gate health from the flake records and derive the reliability bar `verdict.flakes.entry`'s
`measured` value stands for. Once every leg of the bar has thirty verdicts in the flake records, the
threshold is one more than the 90th percentile (cl-dClosedLoop-0339), across the bar's legs, of each
leg's flakes in its last ten verdicts; it is re-derived at each gate-health measurement and written to
the gate-health record with the verdicts it came from, which `test.flakes` reads, two in ten holding
until the record holds one. Today nothing measures a flake rate, and the flake records
`TOOL-dWitnessedBar-8` writes are the only committed catch history (gap-test-10), none tracked yet
(PINNED 2026-11-20, `git ls-files 'memory/builds/*/build/*-flake-*.md'` printing nothing).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The measurement in `tools/drift-audit/drift_report.py`: `read_gate_health` reads every
  tracked flake record (`TOOL-dWitnessedBar-8` S3) through the record factory's `read_records`, plus at
  most one untracked record named by `--with` (S5), and the bar's legs (S2); `derive_gate_health`, a
  pure function of those rows and legs, counts each bar leg's verdicts and its flakes in its newest
  ten by `Ended`; `measure_gate_health(ctx)`, a sibling of
  `drift_report.py#measure_legs_retried_after_timeout` appended to `drift_report.py#SIGNALS`, reports
  the signal `gate_health` with the derived threshold, the legs armed and the legs short. The factory
  is loaded by `load_memory_tree`, which `drift_report.py#load_ledger` becomes a caller of: one
  resolver-and-import for each memory-tree module drift reads (§8 F4). Observed by AC1 and AC3.
- **S2** — The bar's legs: every leg of the gate-leg manifest beside drift's tool root (`gate-legs.json`
  in the parent of the kit's directory, the place `verdict_flow.py#derive_manifest_rel` reads) that the
  kit hold does not hold, the legs a default bar runs (§8 F1). The kit hold is the runner's: `subject`
  `kit` or `chunk` `selftests` (`run-gates.sh#derive_hold_source`). The measurement is armed only when
  each has at least thirty rows (cl-dClosedLoop-0339), and prints `unarmed` naming each short leg and its
  count otherwise. Observed by AC3.
- **S3** — The rule: with `c` the list of each bar leg's flakes in its last ten verdicts, sorted
  ascending, `p90 = c[ceil(0.9 × len(c)) − 1]` (nearest rank, §8 F2), and the threshold is `p90 + 1`
  in ten, written `<count>/10`. A flake is what `derive_flaked` (S7) says. Observed by AC1 and AC2.
- **S4** — The gate-health record: a record kind `gate-health` of the factory, named
  `<date>-build-<unit id>-gate-health-<n>.md`, served as `**Serves:** gate-health <unit id>`, with
  required fields `Measured` (`<count>/10`, shared interface I12), `Verdicts` (the `Run` of every flake
  record the measurement read) and `Legs` (`<armed>/<bar legs>`). The kind joins the reader in the same
  pass: its entry in `build_records.py#KINDS`, and `gate-health` added to `tree_lib.py#TYPED_RECORD_KINDS`,
  which the factory's table must equal at import (PLAY-dOrderedCharter-42 S1, S2), and to the closed
  `<kind>` list of `tools/memory-tree/HYGIENE.template.md` (its S5), whose count of typed kinds after
  `research` moves by one. `python tools/drift-audit/drift_report.py --gate-health --write --build
  <slug> --unit <id>` writes it at each armed measurement through the factory's `write_record`, loaded
  as S1 loads it, and never while unarmed; the kind gets no `new` door, as `flake` and `leg-tail` have
  none (§8 F4). `--gate-health` is a mode of its own, as `--escape-ratio` is: it reads
  `.memory-tree.conf` through the factory, the manifest and the records alone, no project layer and no
  base ref, and is refused beside `--check`, `--offenders`, `--delta` or `--escape-ratio`. Observed by
  AC4 and AC6.
- **S5** — The measurement's moment: under `record` and `quarantine`, once `verdict_flow.py flakes`
  (`TOOL-dWitnessedBar-8` S4) has written its flake record, its `write_gate_health` runs the drift kit,
  found by the kit's inline sibling resolver, as a subprocess in the repository's top: `--gate-health
  --write --build <slug> --unit <id> --with <the record it wrote>`, bounded by `HEALTH_BOUND`. Every line
  the measurement prints is printed unchanged, so its `drift-report: wrote <path>` line is staged by the
  driver's `run_leg_records` with the flake record. No drift kit, a non-zero exit or the bound firing
  prints one `flakes  gate-health · <why>` line, and `flakes` still exits 0. The holds this call prints
  read the threshold in force before it; the new record applies once it is staged. A plain drift report
  reads the measurement and writes nothing. Observed by AC4.
- **S6** — The reader's red case: `verdict_flow.py flakes --check` (`TOOL-dWitnessedBar-8` S7) gains the

### The signal

```python
def measure_gate_health(ctx) -> dict:
    """`gate_health`: the reliability bar derived from the flake records, or `unarmed` naming each
    bar leg short of thirty verdicts."""
    # rows: {leg: [(ended, first_rc, rerun_rc, run), ...]} from every tracked *-flake-*.md, by read_flake_body
    # bar:  manifest legs whose row the kit hold does not hold
    # armed  <=> every leg of bar has len(rows[leg]) >= 30
    # flakes(leg) = count of derive_flaked(first, rerun) in its newest ten rows by Ended
    # value = p90(sorted(flakes(leg) for leg in bar)) + 1
```

It returns `{"signal": "gate_health", "value": <count, or -1 unarmed>, "of": <bar legs>, "armed":
<legs at thirty>, "tolerance": <its pin>, "gateable": False, "live": <the factory and the manifest
read>, "detail": [<per-leg verdicts and flakes>]}`. Unarmed, `value` is -1, so the table prints `n/a`,
and the first `detail` row names the short legs, so a report never reads an unarmed measurement as a
zero. `live` is false, DEAD PROBE, only where no factory resolves or no manifest reads, since the
population moves as verdicts accrue. A record with no readable `Ended`, or one the factory's reader
grades malformed, is skipped and named in `detail`.

The mode prints `drift-report: gate-health measured <count>/10 · legs <armed>/<bar legs> · verdicts
<run id> …`, or `drift-report: gate-health unarmed · <leg> <rows>/30 …`; with `--write` and armed it
then prints `drift-report: wrote <path>`. Exit 0 measured or unarmed; 2 refused, nothing written: no
factory, no manifest, a mode clash, `--write` without `--build` and `--unit`, a `--with` path that is
not a flake record by the factory's name grammar, or a write the factory refuses.

### The record

```
# <unit id> gate-health <n> — measured <count>/10

**Serves:** gate-health <unit id>

- **Measured:** <count>/10
- **Verdicts:** <run id> <run id> …
- **Legs:** <armed>/<bar legs>
```

`build_records.py#write_record` writes the heading and the `**Serves:**` line. `TOOL-dWitnessedBar-8`
reads the newest record by date and `<n>` and takes `Measured`. Each armed measurement writes one, so
the record in force at any date is the newest one dated on or before it, which is what `flakes
--check` reads for a hold's filing date.
