## 1. Goal

gov tolerates its debt in registries of at least six grammars, and drift tracks five of them by a
hand-typed list (gap-test-14, gap-test-15). This unit converts every registry the population rule
finds, in place and in its own home, onto the ledger factory's row grammar, registers each with the
direction its own header declares (`shrink-only` debt, `append-only` history, `declared-set` or
`ceiling`), re-points every reader onto the factory, and retires drift's hand-typed `SHRINK_ONLY`,
`PINS` and `BASELINES` for populations the registrations derive (RF-5; retrofit.md:187-284).

### The conversion

```
# ledger · spawn-idiom-sites · key site path,class · direction shrink-only · drain hunk
.githooks/pre-commit	path-subst	1	<base sha>	seeded
```

One commit converts every file, registers every population, re-points every reader and adds every
`.gitattributes` line, so no tree holds the old grammar beside a new reader. The conversion record
holds, per population, a table of key and former reason, and the count of rows before and after.

The two new files are drift's project layer, as `drift_signals.py` is: withheld from installs by a
`project-owned` `[[files]]` row in `tools/drift-audit/kit.toml`, and an adopter's own `PINS` and
`BASELINES` dicts become its files through S10's step. `derive_low_waters` (`drift_report.py:781`)
replays each list's history by counting its rows; the header is a `#` line, so a regrammar that keeps
one row per key keeps the low water, and the two ordinal registries' low water falls once, at the
conversion, to the keyed count.

`[[upgrade]]` rows of each kit it holds (memory-tree, codebase-map, gate-lint, unattended, lexicon and
drift-audit); a registry its kits never seeded has no row and nothing to convert.
