## 1. Goal

The round-1 spec audit of units 40 to 51
(`memory/builds/dWitnessedBar/reviews/2026-10-25-review-TOOL-dWitnessedBar-40-spec-audit-round1.md`)
confirmed finding **id 43 at HIGH**, its item **H3**, alone in that item. The pre-step readers, the
pre-push hook and the unattended driver, read `INHERITED_RED` with `read_policy_key`
(`.githooks/pre-push@1ecf9aa6:1306-1320`, byte-identical at
`tools/unattended/unattended.sh@f499fbac:9517`), which takes the LAST line beginning exactly
`INHERITED_RED=` at column 0. Neither reads the key at HEAD: `TOOL-dWitnessedBar-3` retired it onto
`verdict.blocks`, and the step `govkit/inherited-red-to-verdict-blocks` must write what they enforced.
`TOOL-dWitnessedBar-44`, landed, has the step's reader, `tools/govkit/steps/inherited_red.py#read_value`,
match lines with another grammar's pattern, `tools/govkit/steps/conf_to_policy.py#build_line_re`. That
reader already takes the last line and never matches an `export INHERITED_RED=` line, but it admits an
indented line and spaces around the `=`, and cuts a BOM, a leading tab and every trailing CR, none of
which the readers did. For a file holding `INHERITED_RED=park` and then `  INHERITED_RED=land`, the
readers parked; the landed step writes `own-mixed-contended`, so MIXED and CONTENDED reds that blocked
yesterday land silently, the loosening unit 44 exists to prevent. Nothing holds the step's reader to the
readers'. This unit takes the report's fix. It repairs `TOOL-dWitnessedBar-44`.

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The reader rule. The step reads a file's `INHERITED_RED` as `read_policy_key` does: per line,
  one trailing CR cut; an assignment is a line beginning exactly `INHERITED_RED=`; the LAST
  assignment wins; its value is cut at the first whitespace character followed by `#`, stripped of
  trailing spaces and tabs and of leading spaces, and stripped of one surrounding pair of double or of
  single quotes. A line in any other spelling, `export INHERITED_RED=`, an indented line, a commented
  one, `INHERITED_RED =` with a space or a first line opening with a BOM, is not an assignment, and a
  leading tab stays in the value. The same rule reads `GATE_POLICY_FILE` from `.unattended.conf`, as
  the driver still reads it (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh#read_gate_policy`). Observed by AC1, AC2
  and AC4.
- **S2** — Empty means undeclared. A last assignment that cleans to empty reads as undeclared, as both
  readers read it (`'')` arms, `.githooks/pre-push@1ecf9aa6:1341-1342`,
  `tools/unattended/unattended.sh@f499fbac:9590-9591`), so it takes unit 44's undeclared row, whatever an
  earlier line assigned. Observed by AC2.
- **S3** — What `up` deletes and `witness` counts. `up` deletes every assignment by S1's rule from each
  file it read, and no other line; `witness` counts every assignment remaining by the same rule. A line
  spelling the key that S1 does not read as an assignment is left as it is and named on the witness
  output as `not an assignment <file>:<line>`, which does not fail it. Observed by AC3.
- **S4** — The rule is held to the readers'. A `tools/govkit/selftest.py` arm runs the step's reader and
  the hook's `read_policy_key`, extracted from `.githooks/pre-push` from its `read_policy_key() {` line
  to the first column-0 `}` and run under the resolved bash, over one fixture set: two assignments, an
  `export` line, a commented line, an indented line, a quoted value, a trailing comment, CRLF line ends,
  an empty last assignment, a later `INHERITED_RED = land` with a space, a leading tab before `land`,
  and a BOM before the first line. Each pair of values is equal. Observed by AC4.
- **S5** — The page says it. `help/upgrade-steps.md`, its section `Where INHERITED_RED's value lives`,
  states that the step reads the last line beginning exactly `INHERITED_RED=` in each file, that the up
  takes out those assignments and leaves every other line, and that the witness names a line in another
  spelling as `not an assignment <file>:<line>` without failing. Observed by AC5.

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- `read_policy_key() { # file text · key -> the LAST <key>= line's value, cleaned; nothing when none
  assigns it`: `line=${line%$'\r'}`; `case "$line" in "$2="*) v=${line#"$2="}; hit=1`; then
  `v=${v%%[[:space:]]#*}`, trailing space and tab stripped, leading space stripped, one pair of `"` or
  `'` removed (`.githooks/pre-push#read_policy_key`, the same bytes at
  `.githooks/pre-push@1ecf9aa6:1306-1320` when it read `INHERITED_RED`).
- The driver's copy carries the comment "TWO COPIES OF THIS PARSER, BYTE-IDENTICAL, and the second is
  the pre-push hook's" above `tools/unattended/unattended.sh#read_policy_key`, and
  `tools/unattended/unattended.sh#read_gate_policy` reads `GATE_POLICY_FILE` with it.
- The pre-step readers mapped an empty value to `land` and anything outside `park land` to `park`
  (`.githooks/pre-push@1ecf9aa6:1339-1343`, `tools/unattended/unattended.sh@f499fbac:9583-9592`).
- The landed step reads through `tools/govkit/steps/inherited_red.py#read_value`: it cuts a BOM, splits
  on LF, cuts every trailing CR, matches `^\s*INHERITED_RED\s*=(.*)$`
  (`tools/govkit/steps/conf_to_policy.py#build_line_re`), drops `\s+#.*$` and strips all surrounding
  whitespace. Its up and witness find the key's lines through `conf_to_policy.py#check_assigned` and
  delete them through `conf_to_policy.py#render_conf_rest`, over the same pattern.
- The step's row `govkit/inherited-red-to-verdict-blocks` is not on `main` at HEAD
  (`git show main:tools/govkit/steps.toml`, PINNED 2026-11-20), and `upgrade_steps.py frozen` reds a
  byte of a program only where the default branch landed its row
  (`tools/govkit/upgrade_steps.py#check_frozen`).

### The change, in the step

`read_value` keeps its signature and takes S1's rule; its BOM cut and its use of `build_line_re` go.
One step-local predicate, `check_policy_assignment`, answers whether a line, one trailing CR cut,
begins exactly `<key>=`, and every read, count and delete of `INHERITED_RED` and every read of
`GATE_POLICY_FILE` goes through it. The up's delete becomes the step-local `render_gate_rest`, which
splits where `read_value` splits and keeps each other line byte for byte, as `render_conf_rest` does.
The witness prints one `not an assignment <file>:<line>` row per line that spells the key and fails
the predicate; `TOOL-dWitnessedBar-62` later defines what spells the key. The module docstring's reader paragraph is restated to S1.

### Inventory

`check_policy_assignment` and `render_gate_rest` in `tools/govkit/steps/inherited_red.py`, each graded
by `.lexicon.conf`'s `py.function` cell, `snake`, and its verb table. Nothing else.

None: no adopter has run the step, whose row `main` does not hold at HEAD, and gov's own

- **Honour `export` lines.** Neither reader does, so the step would map a value nothing enforced.
- **Narrow `build_line_re` to column 0.** It is the grammar of the landed steps `review_rounds.py` and
  `conf_pins_to_ledger.py`, whose confs are sourced, so their readers' rule is not this one.
