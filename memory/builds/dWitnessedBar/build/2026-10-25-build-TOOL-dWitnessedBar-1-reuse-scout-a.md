## Shared interfaces

1. **The verdict record.** File `<git-dir>/gate-run/<run id>/verdict`, `<key>TAB<value>` lines. The runner reads it through `read_record_keys` (`run-gates.sh:2387`), the hook through `check_verdict_record` (`.githooks/pre-push:2268`), drift through `drift_report.py:3126`. New keys, additive with no schema bump (the reasoning at `:3509-3511`):
   - `run_id` and `cause`, unit 25;
   - `tree`, unit 4;
   - `held.<n>` for a held leg's result, units 5, 8 and 30;
   - `warn.<n>` for warnings, unit 29;
   - `tail.<n>` holding a tail's hash, unit 25.
   The indexed-family grammar is runlog's (`tools/runlog/README.md`, "an all-digit suffix is an INDEX").
2. **The per-tree verdict.** Proposed spelling `<common-dir>/gate-verdict/<tree hash>`, written by the runner and read by the hook. It mirrors `gate-full-green.shared`'s placement (`run-gates.sh:5636-5651`). Units 4, 2 and 6. Unit 6's remote verdict carries the same key, "that SHA's tree is the verdict's tree" (layer-expansions:335).
3. **Red classes.** `OWN INHERITED MIXED CONTENDED DEAD PROBE`, the attribution row as written at `run-gates.sh:5064-5067`. Units 3, 8, 23 and 30. The tester (unit 23) judges OWN and blocking MIXED only (flow-steps:360).
4. **The leg key.** The free-text manifest `name` is the join key (cl-0350). Its file-safe form is `heldkeys[$i]` (the `quarantine.<name key>` precedent). Units 1, 5, 7, 8, 25 and 30, and Scout B's unit 12 (`tag` field) must keep `name` as the join key.
5. **The same-tree re-run.** Spelled `runleg "$i" .rerun`, beside `.retry` and `.sample`, writing `<i>.rerun.leg`. Units 8 and 30.
6. **The hold.** One predicate at `run-gates.sh:3129`, extended by a held-source word (`kit`, `quarantine`), and one `GATE held  <leg>  (<source> …)` line. Units 5, 8 and 30.
7. **The flake record** (`record-kind.flake`): leg name, run id, sha, tree, first rc, re-run rc. It is minted through the record factory `build_records.py` (dOrderedCharter-7 S7) once that is built, and registered in `tools/playbook/kinds.toml` with `verdict`, `seen-red`, `attribution`, `routing` and `selection`. Units 1, 8 and 30 (unit 8 is the one writer, flow-steps:361).

9. **The policy keys.** Exactly as v2 spells them: `verdict`, `verdict.blocks`, `verdict.held`, `verdict.tree`, `verdict.red-route`, `verdict.flakes`, `verdict.flakes.entry` (`measured` or `<count>/<window>`), `verdict.flakes.release`, `verdict.remote.trust`, `verdict.remote.pin`, `verdict.remote.pin-scope`, `hooks.bypass`, `trace.leg-output`.
