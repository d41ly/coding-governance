### H1. 90: the BOM-led CRLF plants never carry a CR on a Windows node (id 7)

- **Where:** `2026-10-27-spec-TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-90.md`, section 2 S1 and S3, section 4 "Why a BOM hides a whole file", section 6 AC2.
- **What is wrong:** S1 reads the CI file's bytes at the scanned SHA, and unit 8 S11's fixture commits them to a scratch repository. Git for Windows sets `core.autocrlf=true` at system and global level, so `git add` folds every CR LF to LF in the blob. A probe on this node kept the BOM and dropped every CR.
- **Consequence:** On every Windows node the per-line CR strip is certified without being exercised. A `ci_lines` that strips no CR passes AC1 to AC3, and AC2's red arm (a CR on `- bash: |`) can never fire. Linux CI still exercises it.
- **Fix (judged SOUND):** In S3, require the ci-census fixture's scratch repository to set `core.autocrlf=false` (as `tools/govkit/matrix.py:453` and `tools/branch-reconcile/selftest.py:58` do) or to commit a `.gitattributes` holding `* -text`. Add a precondition to AC1 to AC3: `git cat-file -p <sha>:<ci file> | od -c` shows `\r \n` and the leading `357 273 277` before the census runs, and the criterion fails if either is missing.
- **Left-shift gate:** a fixture-builder check that every scratch repository a selftest commits into sets `core.autocrlf=false` or `* -text`, red on a fixture that writes CR bytes without one.

### H2. 90: `ci_lines` leaves the line-break rule unstated (id 21)

- **Where:** `2026-10-27-spec-TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-90.md`, section 2 S1 and section 5 error states.
- **What is wrong:** S1 says `ci_lines` "strips one trailing CR from every line" but never says what splits a line. YAML treats a lone CR as a break. Python's `splitlines()` also breaks on NEL, U+2028 and U+2029. GitLab's libyaml break test accepts all of these, and other forges' parsers do not agree on them.
- **Consequence:** Split on LF only, a top-level `include: remote.yml` after a lone CR reads as part of the previous job line, 71 S2's file-wide refusal never fires, and the jobs grade `importable`. Split with `splitlines()`, a NEL inside a job line creates a fake column-0 key and misplaces later script lines such as `- npm publish`. Either way a crafted or legacy-Mac file gets a wrong security result.
- **Fix (skeptic's corrected fix; the finder's was judged UNSOUND):** State in S1 that `ci_lines` splits only on LF, strips one trailing CR, and refuses every job of the file, for example `refused: unreadable line break`, when the decoded text holds a lone CR, U+0085, U+2028 or U+2029. That makes the split deny by default for every parser. Plant a `.gitlab-ci.yml` whose `include:` follows a lone CR and one whose `include:` follows a NEL, with both files' jobs reading that refusal. Stage a red with a scratch copy that lacks the check, which turns them `importable`.
- **Left-shift gate:** a bug-class checklist entry: every text reader that grades security names its line-break set, and refuses the characters outside it rather than choosing a split.
