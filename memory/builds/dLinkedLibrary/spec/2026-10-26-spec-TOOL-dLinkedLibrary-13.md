## 1. Goal

A research return is found across builds only through recall or by its file name, and the
`**Serves:** research` binding also covers design records, scout returns, briefs and instruments
records (gap-mem-15). The library round's fold made the discriminator a declared field,
`kind: research-return`, and owed a dated backfill of the existing returns, 72 at `1b759b620` by
`research_count.py` (layer-library `[research-view]`, :244-256). This unit lands that line, its one
reader, the kind's row and check, and the backfill, so the research view (TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-6)
selects returns by what they declare rather than by what they are named.

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- Records carry no front matter: of the 138 research-named records bound `research` at `82a392696`,
  137 open with an H1 and carry the Serves line on line 3, one carries it on line 1, and none carries a
  kind line (measured at writing with a probe over `git ls-files`; PINNED).
- `read_bindings` (`tree_lib.py:471`) scans the first `BIND_HEAD_LINES` = 12 unfenced lines for the
  Serves line and classifies its kind against `RECORD_KIND_TOKENS` (:406), where `research` is one
  token for every research-bound record.
- TOOL-dTotalRecall-42 S5 grades a record's `fields` against the row its Serves kind names, so a
  `research-return` row of form `fields` would grade nothing: the kind's grammar is this unit's.

### The line

```markdown
# Reuse scout A — dLinkedLibrary

**Serves:** research TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-1 TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-2

kind: research-return

The scout's final return, …
```

The line stands in its own paragraph, so a rendered record shows it as one sentence. A design record
or an instruments record named research and written after the cutoff declares its own kind, a
registered name such as `design-record` (TOOL-dTotalRecall-1) or `instrument` (TOOL-dTotalRecall-18),
on the same line.
