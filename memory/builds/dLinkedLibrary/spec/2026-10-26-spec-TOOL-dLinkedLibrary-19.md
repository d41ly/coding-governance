## 1. Goal

A codebase-map dossier is two things in one file: a TOML header the gate reads both ways, and prose
that goes stale, 25 of 31 dossiers holding code newer than their prose when the night pass measured
them (`research-relational-syntax.md:28`). The owner's NP-16 settled what becomes of it, "Header
becomes the node": the header is the `feature.<name>` node of the tag system, and the prose moves
into units and records (night pass `[p4-relations]`, NP-16 at `night-pass.md:536`, :664). This unit
makes each dossier header a member of the `feature` namespace TOOL-dTotalRecall-9 registers, moves
every dossier's prose out, its reuse seams into reuse-entry proposals and the rest into one record per
feature, and gates the dossier so prose does not grow back.

## 4. Design

  & why`, `## Shared seams`, `## Gaps` and `## Reuse affordance`, 370,182 bytes in all; 82 `seam:`

### Alternatives rejected

- **A second extractor for header nodes.** PLAY-dOrderedCharter-19 S2 gives a namespace row one
  `extractor`; two sources of one tag through two rows would be two namespaces.
- **The prose kept, the header merely read as the node.** The clause moves the prose, and stale
  prose beside a live node is the defect the night pass measured.
- **The moved prose in a record per section.** One feature's sections cite each other; one record per
  feature keeps them in one place and one `Prose:` line points at it.
