## 1. Goal

A `gov:note` has no code extent: nothing says where an annotated block ends, and 68% of a feature's
outside spans sit in no derivable definition (gap-mem-31). The decision-to-feature join runs one way:
the `roles` dossier lists 11 spec ids and omits both decision rows that name the feature (gap-mem-32).
Decision rows and bug classes relate to nothing they decide or came from (gap-mem-39). This unit adds
the extractors that emit typed edges from annotations, bug-class front matter, dossiers and decision
rows into PLAY-dOrderedCharter-28 S5's site-adapter table, reads the `decides` TOOL-dLinkedLibrary-22
writes on decision rows, and lands the span rule: a note's span is its definition's extent where one
derives and runs to `gov:end <tag>` only where none does, each malformed pairing refused and seen red
on a planted file, and the span share re-measured with the map's own extractors (night pass, point 4,
`memory/builds/dClosedLoop/build/2026-10-18-build-PLAY-dClosedLoop-1-night-pass.md:249-262`).


### The pairing walk

| In the pairing text | `iter_family` says | Span verdict |
|---|---|---|
| a note whose span derives from a definition | never enters the text | none |
| an open with no later close for its first tag | unclosed open | `unterminated` |
| a close with no open | orphan close | `orphan` |
| two pairs closing in the opposite order to their opens | crossed pair | `crossed` |
| a close naming another tag than the open above it | close naming another name | `mismatched` |
| a pair inside another pair | a span at depth 1 | `nested`, refused here |
| an open, then a close naming its first tag | one span at depth 0 | none; its body read by `read_region` |
