## 1. Goal

Declare each workflow's whole execution shape in `tools/roles/workflows.toml`: its kind, `single`,
`per-unit`, `panel`, `twin` or `experiment`, named as defaults over TOOL-dBoundRoles-22's stage rows,
its checklist and orientation template, its merge, its twin rule and an agent cap bounded by
`runtime.agents-in-flight`, with the roles declaring the kinds they run in. Today the agent set and the
effort are declared while the orientation template and the merge are chosen per harness or prompt,
though aScoutedSeam measured that the shape moves recall and cost (gap-roles-18).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The five kinds as named defaults: `[[kind]]` rows in `tools/roles/workflows.toml`, each
  `kind`, `fan`, `cap`, `merge` and `twin`, per §4 "### The kinds", read by `load_workflow_kinds` in
  `tools/roles/roles_lib.py`. A stage row may omit `fan` and `cap`, and an entry `merge` and `twin`,
  taking its kind's value; `derive_stage_rows(entry, kinds)` is the one place a default is applied, and
  `derive_stage_map` reads its output. Observed by AC1.
- **S2** — The entry's shape fields, each a key of `WORKFLOW_KEYS` (`tools/roles/check_roles.py:92`):
  `kind`, one of the five, required on every entry that lists a role; `checklist` and `orientation`,
  each `none` or an instruction unit tag; `merge`, one of `none`, `concat`, `synth`, `defect-map`;
  `twin`, `never` or the policy key whose value decides a second run; and on a `twin` entry `of`, the
  entry it runs twice. A stage row may carry `kind` where it differs from its entry's. Observed by AC1
  and AC6.
- **S3** — The agent cap bounded: every kind default's `cap`, and every cap a default gives a row, at or
  under `runtime.agents-in-flight` through TOOL-dBoundRoles-22's `read_agent_limit`, the bound that was
  agent-cap's `FANOUT_CAP` before the night pass two's point 7. Observed by AC2.
- **S4** — The experiment kind: a `[workflow.experiment]` table required on an `experiment` entry and
  refused elsewhere, holding `cases`, `variants`, `fan`, exactly one of `scorer` and `judge`, and
  `reruns` keyed by variant and effort, per §4 "### The experiment", the fields aScoutedSeam's benchmark
  measured with and dFairHearing's scorer factory reads. Observed by AC3.
- **S5** — The role field `shapes`, a row of `tools/roles/role-schema.toml`: the kinds a role runs in,
  required on every agent role; R9 reds a stage whose role does not declare the stage's kind; the role
  index carries each role's `shapes` (`render_roles_index`, `tools/roles/render_agents.py:207`).
  Observed by AC4.
- **S6** — The four instruction units the shapes name, `[[unit]]` rows of a new
  `tools/roles/instructions.toml` in PLAY-dOrderedCharter-9 S1's row shape: `unit.orient.producer`,
  `unit.orient.verifier-cold`, `unit.checklist.for-paths` and `unit.checklist.for-diff`, per §4 "### The
  units"; R9 resolves every `checklist` and `orientation` tag through PLAY-dOrderedCharter-9 S2's
  `read_units`, and prints the join `unarmed` where the playbook kit does not resolve. Observed by AC6.
- **S7** — The efforts RS-2 sets: the orientation scout at medium and the builder at high, in the house
  seed `tools/roles/house/policy.toml`'s `[effort.roles]`, in each role's declared `effort` and in the

## 4. Design

### The kinds

```toml
# tools/roles/workflows.toml, above the first [[workflow]] entry
[[kind]]
kind = "panel"
fan = 5
cap = 5
merge = "synth"
twin = "review.twin"
```

| `kind` | `fan` | `cap` | `merge` | `twin` | What it is (systems.md `[design-role-shapes]`, Kinds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `single` | 1 | 1 | `none` | `never` | one agent |
| `per-unit` | 5 | 5 | `none` | `never` | one agent per unit, parallel up to the bound |
| `panel` | 5 | 5 | `synth` | `review.twin` | finders, at most five verifier batches, one synthesizer |
| `twin` | 2 | 2 | `defect-map` | `never` | two runs of the shape `of` names, merged |
| `experiment` | the table's `fan` | the table's `fan` | `none` | `never` | variants over frozen cases, scored |

The fans and caps are PINNED to the harness literals at `4ed9e567` (`SPEC_WRITERS = 5`,
`MAX_VERIFIERS = 5`, the five finder lenses), so a default changes no call. The rows sit above every
`[[workflow]]` entry and are keyed `kind`, never `name`, because the Skill binder in
`tools/roles/adopt-roles.sh:134-160` reads every `name = "` line inside a workflow block.

### The experiment

```toml
[workflow.experiment]
cases = "memory/builds/<slug>/build/<the frozen case set>"
variants = ["base", "house-index"]
fan = 4
scorer = "scouted-seam"
reruns = { "base.medium" = 3, "house-index.medium" = 3 }
```

R9 grades it: `cases` names a tracked file; `variants` is a non-empty list of distinct `seg` words; `fan`
an integer from 1 to the agent limit; exactly one of `scorer`, an `INSTANCES` key of
`tools/workflows/replay_scorer.py` (TOOL-dFairHearing-12 S1) read through
`resolve_kit_dir("workflows", "replay_scorer.py", here)`, and `judge`, a tracked label-key file
(TOOL-dFairHearing-14 S6); and every `reruns` key `<variant>.<level>`, its variant listed and its level
in `EFFORT_LEVELS`, its value an integer of at least 1, each variant keyed at least once. Gov ships no
experiment entry; the kind is what a repository's or a later build's entry declares.
