## 1. Goal

Sort the roster by layer: every role declares its `layer` and whether it `acts-as` an item role or a
cycle role, the twelve core roles exist as role files (four of them new, one the merge of the two
reviewers), the deployer and the maintainer are registered by the expansion layers that own them with
no definition here, and a check keeps every core item to core roles. Today 32 of 70 checklist items are
performed by nobody (gap-roles-01), the main session plays role functions (gap-roles-02), three roles
had no spawn in 60 days and two double up (gap-roles-07), and no check keeps core items to core roles
(gap-roles-14, P28).
