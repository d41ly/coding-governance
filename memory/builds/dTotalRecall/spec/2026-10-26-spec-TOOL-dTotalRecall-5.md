## 1. Goal

`builds.slug-date` and derived once by `derive_slug_grammar(root)` in `tools/memory-tree/id_grammar.py`,
is what every slug and id reader in the tree reads, so an owner can change the grammar in one place
and no reader rejects a slug or id minted under it or before it (gap-mem-06). Today each reader
spells its own pattern: the id-shape census of §4 "The readers" finds 73 lines in 37 files (PINNED
2026-11-22 at `b4137f489`, at `44c6800aa` and at `a8748ffaa`). A digit date breaks six of the readers the slug research probed and a
hyphenated date ten (`research-memory-builds.md:92`), and the one setting that exists,

## 4. Design

### The grammar

```python
SlugGrammar(words=(lo, hi), style, date, node, slug_class, slug_ere, BUILD_SLUG_RE, mint_re, record, fallback)
```

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| `words` | `builds.slug-words`, `<lo>-<hi>` with `1 <= lo <= hi`; default `1-3` |
| `style` | `builds.slug-style`, `humorous` or `descriptive`; default `humorous` |
| `date` | `builds.slug-date`, `none`, `shown` or `in-slug`; default `none` |
| `node` | the conf's node-tag class, `Conf.node_tag_class`, which `_eras` already reads |

One grammar `g` has the shape `<node-letter><date><word>{lo,hi}`, where a word is `[A-Z][a-z]+`, a
capital and at least one lower-case letter after it (§8 F11), `<node-letter>` is one character of
`[<node>]`, and `<date>` is empty unless `g.date` is `in-slug`, when
it is `[0-9]{8}` or `[0-9]{4}-[0-9]{2}-[0-9]{2}`, the digit and the hyphenated form. The date follows
the node letter, so a node's dated slugs sort by date. `style` is the minting source and never a
shape (`layer-memory.md:556`). A word carries no digit, as the slug rule's "`[A-Za-z]` only" states
(`memory/HYGIENE.md@b4137f489:191`), so an undated shape admits only slugs the legacy shape admits:
`dX`, `x` and `dFoo1` fail both, and a digit in a slug is an `in-slug` date and nothing else.

- `mint_re` is `^<shape of the grammar in force>$`.
- The legacy shape is `[<node>][A-Za-z]{2,}`, exactly the session era's slug part today
  (`tools/memory-tree/id_grammar.py#_eras`). It is frozen and admits every slug and id in the tree:
  229 build folders, 227 of two words and 2 of three, all on the strict shape too (PINNED 2026-11-22
  at `b4137f489` and at `44c6800aa`). Of the 286 distinct slugs in the session-era ids
  `grammar_for().ID_RE` finds in tracked `memory/**/*.md` at `b4137f489`, and of the 288 at
  `44c6800aa`, only the fixture slugs `nosuch` and `other` fail the strict one-to-three-word shape
  (PINNED, measured 2026-11-22 at both). Every one of the 3062 distinct ids
  `tools/memory-tree/tree_lib.py#read_bindings` reads at `44c6800aa` fits `slug_class` under the
  defaults (PINNED 2026-11-22), and so do the six slugs of §4 "The flips" under their flips.
- `slug_class` is the alternation of the legacy shape, the shape of the grammar in force, and the
  shape of every distinct grammar a tracked `memory/builds/*/README.md` records in `slug-grammar:`,
  in that order, deduplicated; `slug_ere` is the same alternation in ERE. `BUILD_SLUG_RE` is
  `^(?:<slug_class>)$`, and its printed form is `^(<slug_ere>)$`.

A README recording no `slug-grammar:` is a legacy README and is read under the legacy shape alone.
The recorded grammars are read from the front matter of the READMEs `git ls-files` lists in `root`,
once per process and root, with `tools/memory-tree/tree_lib.py#read_text`; a value that does not
parse as §4 "The record" refuses by path, as the `ConfError` of §4 "The policy reads".

### The two spellings

Every shape is built by one function, `render_slug_shape(g, opener)`, whose one argument beyond the
grammar is the group opener: `(?:` renders `slug_class`, the Python spelling every Python reader
splices, and `(` renders `slug_ere`, the POSIX ERE spelling `--print` prints and every shell, awk and
JavaScript reader splices. Digits are `[0-9]` and repeats are brace intervals in both, so the two
differ in their group openers alone. ERE has no non-capturing group: gawk 5.4 and bash `=~` refuse
`(?:`, and gawk reads `\d` as a plain `d` (measured 2026-11-22, §8 F7). A shell reader splicing
`slug_ere` inside its own groups renumbers its later groups, so the pass reads each such site's
backreferences at its edit.

### The record

`slug-grammar: words=<lo>-<hi> style=<style> date=<date>`, one line in the README's front matter,
written directly after `slug:` by both render functions, each taking it as its last argument.
`SlugGrammar.record` renders it, and `parse_slug_grammar(value)` is its one reader.

### The policy reads

The three keys are read with `read_policy_value("builds.slug-words", root)` and its two siblings, each
the key literal first, the call shape `tools/policy/check_policy.py`'s reader coverage reads.
`tools/memory-tree/tree_lib.py#read_policy_value` runs `policy.py get <key>` in `root`, and `get` reads
`.governance/policy.toml` as committed at the landed revision, the remote ladder's tip or with no remote
the local default branch, never the working tree (`tools/policy/policy.py#derive_landed_revision`).

### The fixtures

Under the defaults `slug_class` is the legacy shape `[a-z][A-Za-z]{2,}`. That is narrower than the
`[A-Za-z0-9]+` and `[A-Za-z]+` slug parts most of the readers above spell today. So it refuses the
fixture slugs suites feed those readers that are one or two letters long, carry a digit, or open
upper-case (`2026-11-22-build-TOOL-dTotalRecall-5-3-builder-return`). Each such slug is re-spelled
(§8 F13).

The population is fixed by two searches over tracked `tools/`, `skills/` and `.githooks/`. The
first finds the id shape and the second the slug contexts:

```bash
git grep -nP -e '(?<![\[A-Za-z0-9_])[A-Z][A-Z0-9]*-(?![a-z][A-Za-z]{2,}-[0-9])[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9]*-[0-9]+(?![A-Za-z0-9_\]])' -e '(?<=[\\][nrt])[A-Z][A-Z0-9]*-(?![a-z][A-Za-z]{2,}-[0-9])[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9]*-[0-9]+(?![A-Za-z0-9_\]])' <rev> -- tools skills .githooks
git grep -nP '(?:builds/|--(?:build|dispatch|slug|origin|preflight)[= ]["\x27]?|"slug": ?"|\bslug=["\x27]|\b[A-Z_]*(?:SLUG|BUILD)[A-Z0-9_]* ?= ?["\x27]?)(?![a-z][A-Za-z]{2,}(?![A-Za-z0-9]))[a-z][A-Za-z0-9]*(?![A-Za-z0-9_-])' <rev> -- tools skills .githooks
```

- **The id search.** It finds an id `<FAMILY>-<slug>-<n>` whose slug the legacy shape refuses, where
  the id follows a non-word character or a `\n`, `\r` or `\t` escape. Its backslash is bracketed,
  `[\\]`, as the census's is: git 2.54.0.windows.1 matched nothing with `\\[nrt]` (measured
  2026-11-22).
- **The slug search.** It finds such a slug in any of these places:
  - a `builds/` segment;
  - the value of `--build`, `--dispatch`, `--slug`, `--origin` or `--preflight`;
  - a `"slug":` or `slug=` value;

**The rule.** A refused slug is re-spelled in this order:

1. Each digit becomes its capitalised English word, so `tFix2` becomes `tFixTwo`.
2. A result the legacy shape still refuses takes the suffix `Fix`, so `x` becomes `xFix` and `tB`
   becomes `tBFix`.
3. A result that the same file already uses as a slug takes a second `Fix`.

Where the slug moves depends on its length:

- **Three or more characters.** It moves at every whole-word occurrence in its file.
- **One or two characters.** It moves only where it appears in an id or a slug context:
  - a `builds/<slug>/` segment;
  - a slug flag's value;
  - a `slug` key, variable or constant;
  - a record heading naming the build;
  - the expected text the suite matches.

  The same letters in any other role stay. Examples are the leg `x` of
  `.githooks/pre-push.test.sh@17f07b292:2687`, the run id `fx-1` of
  `tools/run-gates/keep_tails.py@17f07b292:356`, and the node letter of `a:163` at
  `tools/memory-recall/selftest.py@17f07b292:3231`. A branch name or other occurrence whose role is
  unclear moves only where the suite derives it from the slug.

What the suite builds from a re-spelled fixture:

- A value it derives at run time, such as a `git hash-object` of the planted text, moves with the
  fixture.
- A literal it pins from one, such as a digest, a count or a width, is re-derived by running the
  suite's own derivation. The pass's return names it.
