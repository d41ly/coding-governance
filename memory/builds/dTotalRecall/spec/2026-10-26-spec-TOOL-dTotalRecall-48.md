## 1. Goal

The round-1 spec audit of units 36 to 43
(`memory/builds/dTotalRecall/reviews/2026-10-26-review-TOOL-dTotalRecall-37-spec-audit-round1.md`)
confirmed finding **id 30 at HIGH**, its item **H4**, alone in that item. `TOOL-dTotalRecall-36` S4
writes `**Caused-by:**` on the run record's line after its Serves line and mints `check_cause_line`,
which reds a head with none. `tools/runlog/record.py#check_record_lines` grades the fixed head
`(TITLE, "", None, "", INTRO, "")`, so line 4 must be blank, and `render_markdown` in it writes exactly that
head. The leg `runlog record schema` (`runlog.py check-records`, no guard) grades every tracked run
record, none with a cause line (AC2's figure). Built as written, either every new run record fails the
fixed head, or a widened head leaves `check_cause_line` red on every tracked one. This unit gives the
head one optional cause line and leaves absence to the legacy-aware causes check. It repairs
`TOOL-dTotalRecall-36`.

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The head admits the cause line. `check_record_lines` reads line 4: a line matching
  `**Caused-by:** <target>` is the cause line, graded by `check_cause_line`, and the fixed tail
  `("", INTRO, "")` follows it; a blank line 4 is the head without one, its tail as today. Any other
  line 4, and a second cause line at line 5, is the existing finding, `the record's head is fixed text`.
  The body loop, which starts at `min(6, len(lines))` at HEAD, starts at the head's real length.
  Observed by AC1 and AC3.
- **S2** — Shape, not absence. `check_cause_line` reds a cause whose target is not one of the served
  ids, the Serves tokens with each `N..M` run expanded as `check_serves_line` reads them through
  `UNIT_RANGE_RE`, and never reds a head that has none. Whether a record needs a cause is
  `check_causes`' question, which reads the legacy population. Runlog does not require memory-tree
  (`TOOL-dTotalRecall-5` §4 "The readers"); it reaches that kit only for the slug grammar, with an
  inline fallback, so it cannot rely on that population. Unit 36's runlog arm, which asserts a finding
  on a head with its cause cut, is rewritten here to this rule (§8 F2). Observed by AC2 and AC3.
- **S3** — `render_markdown` writes the cause line `TOOL-dTotalRecall-36` S4 gives it, under the head
  S1 admits. Observed by AC1.

## 4. Design

### The head

```text
1 TITLE
2 (blank)
3 **Serves:** log <ids>          check_serves_line
4 **Caused-by:** <id>  | (blank)  check_cause_line on the first; nothing on the second
5 (blank)              | INTRO
6 INTRO                | (blank)
7 (blank)
```

The INTRO shifts one line when the cause line is present. The body loop starts at the head's real
length, so every finding below the head names its real line. The cause line sits at line 4, inside
the first `tree_lib.py#BIND_HEAD_LINES` unfenced lines `tools/playbook/edge_grammar.py#read_header_edges`
reads as a column-0 `**Caused-by:**`.

### Inventory

Minted: none; `check_cause_line` is `TOOL-dTotalRecall-36`'s, narrowed here and called from
`check_record_lines`.

### Files touched (estimate)

- `tools/runlog/record.py`
- `tools/runlog/selftest.py`

### Alternatives rejected

- **Red an absent cause only for records dated after the seed.** It types the seed's date into the
  runlog kit beside the ledger that owns it, two homes for one fact.
- **Keep the six-line head and put the cause after the INTRO.** Every other writer puts the cause
  line directly under its binding line (`TOOL-dTotalRecall-36` §4 "The head each writer emits"), and a
  run record placed elsewhere is a second placement rule for one line.
- **Leave range expansion and unit 36's arm to `TOOL-dTotalRecall-60`.** §8 F2.
