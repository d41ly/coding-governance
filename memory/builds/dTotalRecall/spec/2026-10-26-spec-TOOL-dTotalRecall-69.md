## 4. Design

### Why this unit stays, and stays after unit 65

Unit 65 consumes unit 60 by a §3 edge, for the reasons unit 60's S26 grades. So unit 60 builds first
in step 31, and no stamp of its could follow unit 65's edit. The first step after unit 65 is 32. The
commit hook makes the stamp unit 65's, and a hook is skippable, so this pass keeps the observation
and the remedy.

### The observation

C5 compares two commits. W is the newest commit touching a watched path. S is the newest commit
that changed the `last-audit` value. W must be an ancestor of S, or equal to it. A bundled stamp makes
them one commit, and its sha then names that commit's parent: `6db81d070` carries `6ab7625a1`, its
parent. AC1 reads the pair and AC2 the stamp's own shape. Neither runs the checker, which is the leg
a role agent's hook refuses. The leg's row carries `pass_leg`, so the main loop's pass legs run the
checker itself after this pass's commit.

At `2ec3df383`, before unit 65, W and S are both `6db81d070` (DERIVED 2026-11-24 by AC1's commands).
Neither read covers C3's clone-depth cases, or a stamp moved by a manifest rename.
