## Where this can go wrong, in one line

S3 is the whole safety property: the verb PROPOSES a class and a person CONFIRMS it. The failure
that matters is a class-3 project fact proposed as a class-1 gov defect, absorbed, and shipped to
every adopter as gov's behaviour. The verb never writes to gov and never writes to the target (S6),
so nothing it gets wrong lands without a human turn.
