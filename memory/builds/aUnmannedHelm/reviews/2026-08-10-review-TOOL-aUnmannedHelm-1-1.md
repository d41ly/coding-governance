## Verdict

**Do not build yet.** Five independent blockers sit on the spec's own load-bearing decisions, and
three of them are collisions between two paragraphs of this same document rather than judgement
calls. First, S1 opts `RUN.md` into hygiene check 8's population while §4 gives Phase a closed enum
that is *deliberately* not check 8's seven-token vocabulary — I extracted the engine's own `nmatch()`
awk and ran it, and every authored row the data model requires (a phase claim, a parked unit, the
mandate line) prints RED, so S1 and §4 cannot both ship. Second, AC4's population guard is the exact
inverse of the `pop_guard` contract §10 cites as its precedent: the real guard passes on an all-zero
population by design, this repo has a recorded gotcha saying the first draft of that guard redded a
freshly scaffolded tree, and no `RUN.md` is tracked today — so a leg built to AC4 reds
`tools/run-gates.sh` on the commit that lands it and reds every adopter on install. Third, §4's
"Files touched" and §10's reuse list still claim `tools/hooks/agent-cap.js`, its test and its wired
copy, which §3 declares out of scope after the F2 fold and which the aNumeralWarden spec already
claims down to the same version constant — the two-specs-one-edit class §3 itself names, live inside
one document. Fourth, that fold moved the modality work out of this build and into nothing: the
receiving spec is untouched at rev-2, carries no dependency stamp, and its backlog row is silent, so
a session can land it CLOSED with the modality refusal never written by anyone and no gate able to
see a missing claim. Fifth, the run's pinned BASE is assigned to a byte-compared generated region
that provably cannot derive it, while the authored region is forbidden from carrying it — the field
AC4's ancestry check and the closing diff review both read from has no legal home. Beneath those sit
22 highs, of which the structural ones are a scope unit (S6, the kickoff hand-back) with zero
acceptance criteria and zero gate legs, a gate-leg budget of +1 against a kit that needs at least
four, an authorization amendment that reaches two of four live statements of the rule, and a mandate
design under which the run authors and commits its own authorization. The design idea is sound and
most of the research is accurate; what is not yet sound is the contract between this master spec and
the engines, specs and charters it touches. That is a rev of spec text, not a redesign — but it must
land before any sub-spec is written, because five of the seven units would be authored against text
that contradicts either the engine or another paragraph of the same file.
