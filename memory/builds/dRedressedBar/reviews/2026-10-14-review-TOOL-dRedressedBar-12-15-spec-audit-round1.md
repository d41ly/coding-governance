## HIGH

### H1. Unit 12 keeps a carried kit's claim but drops its leg ownership, a permanent wedge once that leg's argv changes (id 7)

- **Where:** spec 12, section 3 second non-goal (No carry of `gate_runner.emitted`), section 2 S2, section 4 Alternatives row C5.
- **What is wrong:** The spec says a leg row that a carried kit loses from `emitted` is re-claimed by the same-argv rule (govkit.py:4143-4152) on that kit's next run, "so it wedges nothing". That only holds while gov's resolved argv for the leg is unchanged. `check_leg_claimable` (:4003-4008) needs an exact argv match, and on a mismatch `write_gate_legs` raises a Refusal. `apply` passes no `carry_out_of_scope` (:7953-7955), so a narrower apply drops the other kit's `emitted` rows, while unit 12 now keeps that kit in `kits`. That is the claimed-but-unowned state the engine's own comment calls a wedge.
- **Consequence:** After a narrower `apply` carried kit K, a later gov change to one of K's leg argvs makes every `apply --kits K` exit 2 at LEGS ("already has a leg named X and this target's receipt does not claim it"). Every `update --write` whose scope includes K catches the same Refusal (:11878-11891) and exits 1. Neither verb can rebuild the ownership, so the only way out is hand-editing the target's runner. The C5 rejection as YAGNI rests on the false sentence.
- **Fix (skeptic: SOUND):** Have `apply` pass `carry_out_of_scope=True` to `write_gate_legs` now that it keeps `kits` whole. That is the docstring's rule for a verb whose receipt keeps every claimed kit (:4039-4041). Rewrite S2's docstring sentence and section 3 to match, and add a criterion. Probe for the build (it needs writes, so it was not run here): in a scratch gov and a manifest target, apply two kits that each declare a gate leg, apply one alone, commit a change to the other kit's leg argv, then run `update --write` and `apply --kits <carried kit>`. Without the carry both refuse on "already has a leg named", and with it both pass.
- **Left-shift gate:** a selftest arm that runs the narrower-apply-then-argv-change sequence above, and a bug-class checklist entry: "a verb that carries a kit's claim must carry its leg ownership too".
