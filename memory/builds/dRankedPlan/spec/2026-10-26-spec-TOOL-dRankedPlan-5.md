## 1. Goal

v1 tabulates, for each live build outside the chain, the chain build that absorbs it, and v2 keeps
that table with one change: no chain build waits for a live build to close, the active builds finish
on their own track, and "the Plan build carries the mapping out with the owner's ratification and
confirms dPolishedVitrine's track" (v2:1787-1792; v1:147-177). Nothing records the mapping today, so
no reader can tell a live build the chain absorbs from one it leaves alone. This unit carries the
clause `the live-build mapping carried out`: one owner-ratified record per live build, kept or
absorbed, and the plan view showing each.

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The population: every build `PLAN.md` lists at the build run's base, its placed rows and
  its `## Unplaced` bullets (TOOL-dRankedPlan-1 S3), less the 22 chain builds of v2's `[chain]` table,
  derived by §8 F1's probe at that base and never typed. At `0689e7fa1` it is v1's 24 rows plus
  `aScoutedSeam` and `dClosedLoop`. A build v1 maps that is no longer live is not recorded. Observed
  by AC1.
- **S2** — The mapping per build, §4 "The mapping": `absorbed` for each v1 row whose absorbing chain
  build v1 names without "after it closes", `kept` for the four builds the owner named to finish on
  their own track (R3.3) and for every build v1 does not map. Observed by AC2.
- **S3** — The record per build: one disposition record (`record-kind.disposition`,
  TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-16 S4) in that build's own `build/` folder, named under its first unit id,
  written by the factory's door with `--item item.entry.place-owner`, `--disposition amend` for
  `absorbed` or `continue` for `kept`, and a `Reason` of §4's form. `item.entry.place-owner` declares
  the placement set (TOOL-dPlacedRequest-3 S2), so `check_disposition_record` grades each value.
  Observed by AC1, AC2.
- **S4** — The ratification. Before the first record, `python tools/loop/ratify.py dRankedPlan`
  (`read_plan_ratification`, TOOL-dRankedPlan-9, I6) is read. With a witness, every record's
  `Channel` is `prompt` under `request` and `hook-keyword` and `terminal` under the other two values,
  and its `Reason` ends `ratified <value> <witness>`. With none, no record is written and the unit is
  held for the owner, who writes the records at a terminal. Observed by AC3.
- **S5** — The confirmed track. `dPolishedVitrine`'s record, and the record of any kept build that
  `gen_build_index.py --dormant` (TOOL-dRankedPlan-4 S2) names, carries `dormant since <date>` in its
  `Reason`, so the track is confirmed with its dormancy stated and its disposal left to the owner's
  `dispose` (TOOL-dRankedPlan-4 S3). Observed by AC4.
- **S6** — The plan view shows it. `derive_mapping(build)` in `tools/memory-tree/gen_build_index.py`
  reads the build's newest disposition record whose `Item` is `item.entry.place-owner`, and
  `render_plan_view` appends ` · kept` or ` · absorbed by <slug>` to the build's `## Unplaced` bullet,
  or to its row's `Status` cell when the build is placed. A build with no such record renders as
  TOOL-dRankedPlan-1 renders it. Observed by AC5.

## 3. Non-goals (OUT)

- Moving any mapped build. An absorbed build is not ended: its own units stay as they are, the chain
  build covering its area builds what the design needs, and a concurrent change is reconciled at
  Test's prepared merge (v2:1789-1790, FI-3). Ending one is the owner's `dispose`
  (TOOL-dRankedPlan-4).
- Rewriting a mapped build's specs, README or backlog: each gains one record in its `build/` folder,
  and its README's generated records region re-renders.
- Placing a kept build in the plan: its `priority:` is the owner's or the planner's under
  `plan.authority` (TOOL-dRankedPlan-1, -2), and `plan.unplaced` still judges it.
- The chain builds themselves, which v2's `[chain]` table maps.
