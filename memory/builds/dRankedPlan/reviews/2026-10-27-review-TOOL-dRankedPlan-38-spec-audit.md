- **What:** S10 sends a heredoc body to read_python_callers only when the command word is literally `python`, `python3` or `py`, and to read_shell_callers only for literal `bash` or `sh`. The ban passes the form S10 exists to refuse, and AC8 cannot pass. The tree's own `"$PY" -` and `"$PYBIN" -` heredocs stay unread.

A body fed to `bash`, `sh` or `$BASH` is read by read_shell_callers, matched as its own command-word test rather than through 'an interpreter'.
