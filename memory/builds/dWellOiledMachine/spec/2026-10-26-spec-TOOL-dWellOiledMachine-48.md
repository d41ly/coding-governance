## 1. Goal

A question gov leaves to the owner waits today until a person happens to read it: nothing carries a
deadline, nothing decides it when the owner stays silent, and no channel reaches an owner who has no
session open (gap-rt-18, gap-rt-38). This unit makes every such question an owner item carrying
`run.owner-deadline`, decided past it as `run.on-owner-silence` says, the recommended option by
default and recorded with the deadline as its witness, never deciding a ratification or an owner-only
write and never applying a loosening by silence; and it pushes each item through `notify.channel`
while no owner session is open, as `notify.owner-items` says, with a core-only install reporting that
it has no push channel instead of refusing the default. It also ships the lifecycle's
`life.notify` step, the notice a withdrawn or superseded build posts to its dependents (v2:1227-1230,
the stall-and-loop record's item 10, the owner's SL-4, SL-5, SL-6 and FK-4-1).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The owner item (I28),
  `raise_owner_item(kind, build, options, recommended, change=None, flow_change=False, attended=False, key="")` in
  `tools/runtime/owner_items.py`, and its verb
  `python owner_items.py raise <kind> <build> --option <name>… --recommend <name> [--change <key>=<value>] [--flow-change] [--attended] [--key <text>]`.
  It posts one mailbox `question` through
  `unattended.sh --mail post <build> --to project --kind question --subject "owner item <kind> <build> <key>" --item owner.<kind> --default <recommended> <body file>`
  (KICK-dKeptSession-5 S5, KICK-dKeptSession-24 S2, KICK-dKeptSession-28's `project` recipient), the
  body the `key: value` lines of §4 "The item". It prints the I28 line

  `run.owner-deadline` hours. A second raise with the same kind, build and key is the mailbox's
  duplicate (KICK-dKeptSession-22) and exits 0 printing `owner item — already open · <id>`. A kind
  outside `OWNER_ITEM_KINDS`, a `--recommend` not among the options, and no option are refused, exit

- **S2** — Which questions become items: `python owner_items.py ask <kind> <build> [--attended] …`

- No `run.on-owner-question` resolution: TOOL-dBoundRoles-11 S5 answers it, and S2 only raises.
- No notification adapter beyond the terminal: a registered channel is an integration's row.
- No `progress —` line or `notify.owner`: KICK-dKeptSession-11's, and progress is never pushed.
- No `owner item —` row in the charter's micro-format fence: the line is a verb's output, and the
  owner's turn-end lines stay KICK-dKeptSession-11's.
- No resumption of a parked build when the owner answers later: the producer reads the answer.

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- No owner item exists: `git grep -n "owner item" -- tools` finds no producer, and the only owner hold
  is `run_hold`'s `owner-decision` code (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh:1029`), which waits for a person
  with no deadline.
- The four keys are rows already: `notify.channel` (`tools/policy/catalogue.toml:549`, an enum of
  `terminal` with `registry = "another registered channel"`), `notify.owner-items` (:555),
  `run.owner-deadline` (:561, `direction = "raise-loosens"`) and `run.on-owner-silence` (:568);
  `run.on-owner-question` (:574) is dBoundRoles'.
- `read_catalogue_row` (`tools/policy/policy.py:219`) reads a row's `direction`, and `check_direction`
  (:307) holds every loop bound and deadline to `raise-loosens` (TOOL-dGroundRules-11), so a raise of
  such a key is the loosening v2:1230 names.
- `verb_park` (`unattended.sh:12872`) prints `already parked, unchanged` on a second park (:12904), so
  a park repeated by a second node changes nothing.

### The item

The question's body, one `key: value` line each, then a blank line and the free text:

```
kind: <a member of OWNER_ITEM_KINDS>
build: <slug>
options: <name>+<name>…
recommended: <name>
loosens: yes | no
change: <key>=<value> | none
attended: yes | no
deadline-hours: <run.owner-deadline at the raise>
```

`OWNER_ITEM_KINDS` ships `fork`, `owner-gate`, `ratification`, `owner-write` and `approval`, the
kinds `owner-question` reads plus an approval of a change. The item's states are the mailbox's
(KICK-dKeptSession-5 S7) read through this unit: `open`, `held` when acked by a park, `answered` with
the owner's answer, and `decided` with an answer whose body opens `decided`.

### The silence table

Past `run.owner-deadline`, for an item still `open`:

| `run.on-owner-silence` | An ordinary item | An owner-only kind | A loosening approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| `apply-recommendation` (default) | decided: its recommended option | one reminder through the channel, then park | decided: `decline` |
| `remind-then-park` | one reminder through the channel, then park | the same | the same |
| `wait` | park, no reminder | park, no reminder | park, no reminder |

A park keeps the item unanswered and releases the build's lease, so a later owner answer is still
read. One reminder is the push of S7 with the line `owner item — reminder · <id> · parked at
deadline`, sent whatever `notify.owner-items` says unless its value is `turn-end-only`.

### Alternatives rejected

- **A new ref namespace for owner items.** It would repeat the mailbox's append-only commits, states,
  sightings and duplicate check, and open a write surface the record door does not cover.
- **Deciding at the raise when the owner is away.** FK-4-1 holds every item until its deadline, and
  an item raised while attended is held the same way.

## 5. Production-readiness checklist

- security — the owner's answer needs the terminal proof; every push is secret-scanned under
