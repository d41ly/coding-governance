## 1. Goal

Promoted from the spec audit of the round-2 promotions
(`memory/builds/dWellOiledMachine/reviews/2026-10-27-review-TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-80-spec-audit.md`),
finding 25, item H3, at HIGH. TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-90 S1 widens `design_lib.read_spec_writes`
itself to return every §7 `New arm:` suite path. That function is also "the one token read" of
`tools/check-spec-tokens.py`'s `extract_files_touched`, which feeds the guard-legs join, and
`memory/TEMPLATE-SPEC.md` states that the `New arm:` prefix "is exactly what keeps it out of the leg
join". Built as written, every live spec whose arm path trips a narrow guard its leg line omits turns
`spec tokens` red, and adopters of the copy-installed memory-tree kit read differently on their next
update. This unit puts the arm paths into the build-order write set in its one caller, through one
reader of `New arm:` lines that the spec-token checker's covers join shares, and leaves
`read_spec_writes` reading Files touched alone.
