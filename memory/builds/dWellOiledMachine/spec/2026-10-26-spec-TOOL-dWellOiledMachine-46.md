## 1. Goal

Activity is read as progress today: the resume cap counts consecutive fruitless launches and resets on
any transcript entry (`tools/unattended/resume-tick.sh:71-74`), so an act that writes and never
finishes relaunches without end (gap-rt-37), and a holder that loops while active keeps its lease.
This unit keeps a progress ledger per item instance (I14): each act records whether it produced a new
witness, a new commit on its unit, a changed findings digest, or a refusal and its rule.
`runtime.no-progress` acts with no new witness make the item stuck, a repeat of the act before's
verdict digest or refusal rule counting as one, both detectors journaled and routing nothing until a
replay over gov's recorded run logs records their false positives; a wait inside its declared
deadline is exempt; the relaunch cap counts lifetime relaunches, reset only by a new witness; an
instance reaped twice at the act ceiling routes to its producer to split; and a build's lease renews
under `leases.renewal`'s `progress` on witnessed progress and on declared waits inside their deadline,
never on activity, the supervisor's beat renewing nothing (P39, the parked debt's review, the owner's
FK-2-4).

## 2. Scope (IN)

- **S1** — The ledger, `record_progress(slug, key, act, kind, outcome)` in `tools/runtime/progress.py`,
  appending one tab-separated row to `<git-common-dir>/gov/progress/<slug>.tsv`: `t`, `key` (the I14
  instance key, TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-7 S3), `act`, `kind` (`exit`, `reap`, `relaunch` or `routed`),
  `witness` (the item's witness commit or `-`), `unit-commit` (the unit branch's tip or `-`), `digest`
  (the return's findings digest or `-`), `rule` (the refusal's rule or `-`), `wait` (an open wait id
  or `-`) and `new` (`yes` or `no`). `new` is `yes` when the witness differs from the instance's last
  recorded one, the unit commit moved, or the digest differs from the act before's; a refusal, an
  unchanged digest and a moved transcript are `no`. The supervisor (TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-6 S4) calls
  it at each act's exit, and at each reap TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-47 S4 journals. Observed by AC1 to
  AC4.
- **S2** — The repeat detector: an act whose digest, or whose refusal rule, equals the act before's on
  the same instance is a repeat, journaled `repeat` through `write_journal_line`
  (TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-16 S3) with `act=`, `key=` and `of=digest` or `of=rule`; its row is `new:
  no`. Observed by AC2.
- **S3** — The no-progress detector: when the instance's `new: no` rows since its last `new: yes` row
  reach `runtime.no-progress` (`tools/policy/catalogue.toml:451`, read through `resolve`), the item is
  stuck, journaled `no-progress` with `act=`, `key=` and `count=` once per instance and count. An act
  whose row names an open wait still inside its deadline, `check_deadline_passed` (TOOL-dPlacedRequest-17
  S4) reading rc 1 on that wait's declaration commit (TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-18 S1), is not counted, so
  a poll is no loop. Observed by AC2, AC3, AC7.
- **S4** — Journal-only until the replay: `DETECTORS_ROUTE = False` in `progress.py`, so S2 and S3
  journal with `route=none` and route nothing. `python progress.py replay <record>…` reads gov's
  recorded run logs, the committed run records `memory/builds/*/build/*-runlog-*.md`, taking each
  Timeline `dispatch` row of a unit as one act of its instance and a commit whose subject names that
  unit, read through `git log --grep`, as its new witness; it prints, per record, each instance the
  detectors would mark at the key's value and whether that unit's spec later reached `CLOSED`, a false
  positive when it did. `--write` records the totals in
  `memory/builds/dWellOiledMachine/build/<date>-build-TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-46-progress-replay.md`,
  `**Serves:** research TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-46`. Observed by AC8, AC9.
- **S5** — The lifetime relaunch cap. TOOL-dWellOiledMachine-6 S5's count of an instance's relaunches
  reads this ledger: the `relaunch` rows since the instance's last `new: yes` row, so its life is
  counted and only a new witness resets it. `derive_attempts` (`tools/unattended/resume-tick.sh:228`)
  counts the same way for the tick's stalled-run resume, and its header comment at `:71-74` is
  rewritten to say a transcript entry resets nothing. Past `RESUME_ATTEMPTS` the instance is routed
  (S7): no park and no owner item. Observed by AC1.
- **S6** — Reaped twice: an instance with two `reap` rows and no `new: yes` row between them is routed
  to its producer to split (S7), and the supervisor launches no third act on its key. Observed by AC4.
- **S7** — The route to the producer, `route_to_producer(slug, key, cause)`: the backward row of
  TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-13's build table from the build's state, B8 (`item.design.reentry`) from
  `building`, B16 (`item.test.route`) from `testing` and B17 (`item.deploy.current`) from `deploying`,
  recorded through `write_transition` (TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-3), whose backward direction draws
  `route-back` on the build's rework budget (TOOL-dDeclaredLoop-30 S4); from `planning` or
  `designing`, which have no such row, `record_draw(build, "re-dispatch", item)` alone. Either writes
  the `routed` row, after which the supervisor launches nothing more on that key. Observed by AC1, AC4.
- **S8** — Renewal on progress. `--lease renew` under `leases.renewal` `progress`
  (TOOL-dPlacedRequest-1 S8, whose witness rule hands the progress ledger to this build) gains two
  witness arms in `tools/unattended/unattended.sh`: a commit a `new: yes` row of this build's ledger
  names, and the declaration commit of an `open` wait of this build that `check_deadline_passed` reads
  rc 1, which renews even when it equals the record's `witness`, at most once per
  `WAIT_RENEW_SECONDS` (3600). The supervisor renews at each `new: yes` row and, while an open wait of
  its build is inside its deadline, at that interval; its beat (`write_claim_beat`,
  `unattended.sh:2469`) writes `renews: no`, and a fence beat with no new witness renews nothing
  (TOOL-dPlacedRequest-17 S6). So a holder active without a witness loses its lease after
  `leases.expiry-hours`. Observed by AC5, AC6, AC7.
- **S9** — `help/runtime.md` gains a progress section: the ledger's fields, what counts as new, the two
  detectors and their journal-only state, the lifetime cap, the route and the renewal rule. Observed by
  AC10.
- **S10** — The fixture, `tools/runtime/progress_fixture.py --case <name>`: a bare remote and two
  clones under `%TEMP%` with short names, a build at a planted state with a build lease, a stub
  `claude` that writes a transcript entry every turn and exits at `--max-turns` with or without a
  witness, a planted findings digest or refusal per act, and `runtime.no-progress`,
  `leases.expiry-hours` and `runtime.act-ceiling` planted per case. It prints the ledger rows, the
  journal lines, the transitions and the lease's verdict. Observed by AC1 to AC8.

## 4. Design

### Evidence

- The cap is consecutive and resets on a transcript entry: "Only the sidecar lines newer than the
  run's last move count against RESUME_ATTEMPTS, so a resume that produced a commit, a write or a
  transcript entry resets the count" (`tools/unattended/resume-tick.sh:70-74`), in `derive_attempts`
  (:228), the last move read by `derive_last_move` (`tools/unattended/unattended.sh:8669`).
- The lease grammar hands this unit the witness: "Until dWellOiledMachine's progress ledger lands, the
  transition record is the only witness" (TOOL-dPlacedRequest-1 S8), and `leases.renewal`'s row says
  `progress` is "armed by dWellOiledMachine's progress ledger" (v2:1159).
- `runtime.no-progress` is a row (`tools/policy/catalogue.toml:451`, a retry count, default 3,
  `raise-loosens`).
- 22 committed run records match `memory/builds/*/build/*-runlog-*.md`, PINNED at this base on
  2026-10-26 and re-derived by `git ls-files` at the replay; each carries a Timeline of `dispatch`
  rows per unit.

### Data model

```
t	key	act	kind	witness	unit-commit	digest	rule	wait	new
1761480000	item.build.core@1.2#a1b2	act-0123456789ab	exit	-	9f3e…	c4d5e6	-	-	yes
1761483600	item.build.core@1.2#a1b2	act-1a2b3c4d5e6f	exit	-	9f3e…	c4d5e6	-	-	no
```

The ledger is node-local and derived only from what the supervisor saw on this node; a take-over
starts the instance's count from the witnesses on the remote, never from another node's ledger.

### Alternatives rejected

- **Counting progress from the transcript.** The transcript is activity, the very signal the clause
  stops trusting.
- **Arming the detectors in this unit.** The clause keeps them journal-only until a replay records
  their false positives, and how many false positives are tolerable is the owner's call.

## 5. Production-readiness checklist

- security — the ledger is node-local and read only by the supervisor; renewal arms admit only this
  build's own ledger rows and waits.
- perf / scale — one appended row per act; the count reads one file.
- error / empty / loading states — an unreadable ledger counts as empty, so the cap is never loosened
  by a read failure that later resets; the replay names each record it could not parse.
- observability — the `repeat` and `no-progress` lines, the `routed` row and its transition.
- risks — a long legitimate act with no witness until its end can be counted twice if reaped; the
  route then asks for a split, which is the clause's outcome.
- testing — the fixture cases under AC1 to AC8 and the arm in §7.
- migration — the tick's cap changes meaning: a run mid-stall keeps its sidecar, now counted since its
  last witness.
- user docs — `help/runtime.md`.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

Each case runs `python progress_fixture.py --case <name>` in its scratch clones.

- **AC1** — When `--case transcript-loop` runs an instance whose stub writes its transcript every turn
  and never finishes or witnesses, relaunches stop at `RESUME_ATTEMPTS` counted over its life, a
  `routed` row is written, the transition record names B8 with a `route-back` draw, and no park and no
  owner item is written.
  Red when: the planted act that writes its transcript every turn and never finishes is relaunched
  past the cap, as a count that a transcript entry resets shows, or is parked.
- **AC2** — When `--case repeat-raised` plants five acts repeating one findings digest under
  `runtime.no-progress` 5, four `repeat` lines are journaled and one `no-progress` line at the fifth.
  Red when: the planted repeat under a raised `runtime.no-progress` is stuck before that count, or not
  at it.
- **AC3** — When `--case exact-count` runs three acts with no new witness under the default 3, the
  third journals `no-progress · count=3` and the second journals none.
  Red when: an item at exactly that many acts with no new witness is not stuck.
- **AC4** — When `--case reaped-twice` reaps one instance twice at `runtime.act-ceiling` with no new
  witness, a `routed` row follows the second reap and no third `act-launch` line is written for its key.
  Red when: an instance reaped twice is relaunched instead of routed.
- **AC5** — When `--case active-loop` runs a holder whose acts commit and write transcripts but never
  witness, with `leases.expiry-hours` planted at `0.0005` and the supervisor beating, `--claims` reads
  the build lease `stale` after it, and every beat record carries `renews: no`.
  Red when: the planted holder that loops while active keeps its lease after `leases.expiry-hours`.
- **AC6** — When `--case red-pushes` runs fix passes that keep pushing one red verdict through the
  fence, each push's lease record carries `renews: no`, and the lease reads `stale` after
  `leases.expiry-hours`.
  Red when: a fence beat with no new witness renews the lease.
- **AC7** — When `--case wait-exempt` plants an open wait inside its deadline on the instance, its acts
  add no count and the lease renews once per planted interval; past the wait's deadline the next act
  counts and no further renewal is written.
  Red when: a wait inside its deadline is counted, or a wait past it renews.
- **AC8** — When `--case journal-only` reaches the no-progress count, the journal line carries
  `route=none` and no transition is written.
  Red when: a detector routes before its replay.
- **AC9** — When `python progress.py replay` runs over two planted run records, one whose unit reran
  three times and then reached `CLOSED`, it prints one would-be `no-progress` instance marked a false
  positive.
  figure: the counts are DERIVED at each run from the records given.
  Red when: the replay marks a unit that later closed as a true stop.
- **AC10** — When `grep -n "runtime.no-progress" runtime.md` runs over the help page, it names the
  section, and the page names `leases.renewal`, `progress` and `leases.expiry-hours`.
  Red when: the key or the renewal rule is absent.
