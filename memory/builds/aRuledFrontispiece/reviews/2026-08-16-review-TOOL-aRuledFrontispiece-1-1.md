## Verdict: BLOCKED

**Serves:** spec-audit TOOL-aRuledFrontispiece-1..11  <!-- round 1 of 3 over the whole spec set -->

M4 spec audit of the ten-spec set, four lenses plus this synthesis pass, every BLOCKER and HIGH
re-verified against the tree at this worktree's tip. The set is BLOCKED: seven defects make at least
one unit unbuildable from its own document as written, and three of them are not local slips but
holes in the union of the ten scopes. The largest is that no unit ever inserts the three new
generated marker pairs into any README — units 2, 3 and 4 each ship a region that is opt-in by
presence and each names unit 10's retrofit as the owner of the insertion, and unit 10 does not carry
it — so three of ten units would land renderers, refusals and arms with zero call sites in the
corpus, and the build's stated acceptance ("everything else is rendered from the sources that
already own it") would hold for zero files including its own README. Beside it sit two ordering
defects that cannot all be satisfied at once (the README's `#` column, spec-6's Rollout and
spec-10's Rollout each name a different sequence for the leg and the retrofit), a wrap in unit 5
that reds hygiene check 9 for four consecutive units against its own AC1, a retrofit that creates
eight fresh slot violations it never repairs, and an S2 that builds the roster option the owner did
not pick. The lenses also cleared a great deal: the marker-pair names, the verb spellings, the
`HDR_RE` and check-12 unanchored-prefix claims, the whole of spec-8's five-fact inventory, spec-7's
twelve-site enumeration and spec-4's entire records census all reproduce exactly, and the set's
reuse audits do not overstate what the corpus lacks.

Legend: `spec-N` is `TOOL-aRuledFrontispiece-N`; `(order K)` is that unit's position in the build
README's Units table `#` column, which is not the id sequence.

## Evidence for the blockers and the highs

Each claim below was re-run in this worktree rather than taken from a lens.

**No unit inserts the three new pairs.** `grep -rn 'gen:build-order\|gen:build-edges\|gen:build-docs'`
over the build folder hits exactly three files: spec-2 (6 lines), spec-3 (2), spec-4 (6). The same
grep over spec-10 returns 0. Spec-10's S2 is two operations — the roster wraps and the six prose
moves — and its Files touched names neither a marker nor a declaration for the new regions. The
deferrals are at spec-2 §3 ("retrofitting any existing README (unit 10)"), spec-3 §3 ("Declaring
edges across the corpus. Unit 10 owns the retrofit") and spec-4 §3.

**Unit 5's wrap reds check 9.** Four tracked build READMEs carry a `**Build status:**` line over 300
characters: aRuledFrontispiece 354, aSealedCaravan 327, aUnmannedHelm 331, cBriefedPilot 577. S5
wraps at 300, so those four files' committed bytes stop equalling a fresh render. Check 9 is
`gen_build_index.py --check` at `tools/memory-tree/check-memory-hygiene.sh:464`. Spec-5 §3 puts
"Re-rendering the corpus" OUT and §4 Migration says the re-render "is unit 10's commit rather than
this one's", while AC1 requires the hygiene gate to exit 0 at unit 5's tip. Unit 5 is order 6 and
unit 10 is order 9, so the bar stays red across four consecutive units — the M6 violation the
README's ordering section moved unit 8 to second in order to avoid. Spec-5 §4 also says the wrap
"re-renders exactly three"; it is four at the build's own tip.

**Three sequencings for the leg and the retrofit.** The README's Units table puts unit 10 at 9 and
unit 6 at 10, and its ordering section rejects folding the five relocations into the leg's commit.
Spec-6 §4 Rollout does exactly that ("This unit performs those five moves in the commit that adds
the manifest row"), then leaves the split conditional while recording §8 "none". Spec-10 §4 Rollout
says "Three commits, in this order, all after units 1 through 6 have landed" and §5 expects "the
format leg from unit 6 running over the real corpus for the first time at commit C" — both put unit
6 before unit 10. No document names the loser.

**The retrofit creates eight new slot violations.** Thirteen tracked build READMEs carry a roster or
units heading, which reproduces spec-10's count. In ten of them at least one authored heading sits
between that heading and the generated open: aDrainedSluice 1, aFoldedQuarry 1, aMooredAnchor 2,
aStandingWrit 4, aUnmannedHelm 2, aWrittenMethod 4, cBriefedPilot 3, cFinalBerth 2, cKeyedLaunchpad
7, cSteadyMetronome 3. Spec-10's six-row relocation table names only two of the ten. Concretely,
aDrainedSluice's `## Order` section sits between its Units table and its generated open, and
cFinalBerth has two such sections. Operation 1 therefore manufactures eight violations operation 2
cannot see, because operation 2's population was measured before the pairs existed. Unit 6's AC6
requires `--check-format` to exit 0 over every folder under the builds directory.

**S2 builds the roster option the owner did not pick.** Fork 1 resolved to "Option A split — the
authored PLAN sits inside a `roster:units` marker pair, the DERIVED state renders outside it".
Spec-10 S2 wraps "that table" as it stands and §3 forbids editing it. Candidate tables carry live
mutable state: cBriefedPilot's Units table has a `State` column reading MISSING/READY, and
aDrainedSluice's carries `— pending` per row. The driver's `check_authorization` byte-compares that
slice against the pinned BASE at `tools/unattended/unattended.sh:493-505` and its `fail 20` has no
override; the sibling build's own spec-7 §8 records Option B as "a wedge with no exit". Twelve
READMEs would gain a region frozen for a run's whole life around state the build method orders a run
to update.

**S4 reverses unit 1's S1a.** Spec-1 S1a: "the slot refusal is NOT reachable from `plan()`,
`--write` or `--check`". Spec-10 S4: "A mis-bounded README stops the render", with AC3 "`--check`
fails" and AC4 "`--check` refuses". The mechanism S4 names is the records-sentence remover's
anchor/sentence split (`RECORDS_SENTENCE` and `RECORDS_ANCHOR` at `gen_build_index.py:392-393`, two
refusals at :448-461), but a prose block bounded by POSITION has no anchor, so "an anchor the
bounding pattern does not fully match" has no referent and AC4's opened-and-not-closed shape
describes the marker-delimited block spec-1 §4 explicitly rejected. Spec-10 §5 then calls this "the
single highest-risk item in this build. It writes 39 authored files in one commit", while S2, §4 and
§10 all say the restructure is hand work over six files with no tool.

**`apply_region`'s signature has a second caller.** Repo-wide there are two:
`gen_build_index.py:583` and `tools/memory-tree/marker-contract.test.sh:69`, which calls
`G.apply_region(t, ..., path)` positionally from an inlined reader. A required new parameter raises
TypeError, which is not the module's `Problem`, so the shim crashes and every case scores an error
verdict no `want` value matches. Spec-1 §7 names that leg as a gate to keep green and §4 Files
touched omits it. The same shim hardcodes the build-index markers at its lines 44-45, so §7's
justification — "a second marker pair enters the contract that test drives" — is false; the roster
pair never enters the four-reader contract in this build.

**Spec-7's AC2 is truth-blind.** Ran verbatim:
`git grep -n 'STATUS\.md' -- tools/ memory/HYGIENE.md memory/README.md .gitattributes` returns 13
hits, and from the hygiene engine only lines 284, 422 and 431. The four sites S3 and S4 name spell
the path as a shell regex and are invisible to that pattern: line 134 (the precondition), 331
(the index-set member), 430 and 432 (the check-8 selectors). AC1 does not cover the gap either,
because the backlog shards keep that population non-zero whether or not the selectors were removed.
The unit can be declared done with three of its twelve enumerated sites untouched.

**The aStandingWrit premise.** That build's spec header reads `CLOSED · rev-6 · ... · ratified`,
while `memory/backlog/TOOL.md:18` still says SPECCED — the row spec-1 §4 read. The code is live:
`ROSTER_OPEN`/`ROSTER_CLOSE` at `tools/unattended/unattended.sh:94` and the BASE comparison at
:493-505. Nothing in the ten specs touches a backlog shard, so the stale row also survives the build.

**AC10 cannot fail.** Both tracked run-state files are `phase: LANDED` (aSealedCaravan line 19,
aSiftedPlaybook line 24) and unit 8, at order 2, skips check 8's equality refusal at a terminal
phase. Unit 5's wrap does change the compared region for aSealedCaravan, whose status line is 327
characters. So AC10 reports green after the region changed.

**The kickoff ratchet.** `memory/guides/SESSION-KICKOFF.md:6` watches the hygiene engine, the gate
manifest, the memory-tree conf and the build method. Only spec-9 (order 8) re-stamps. Unit 10 (order
9) commit B edits the hygiene engine; unit 6 (order 10) adds a gate manifest row. C5 at
`skills/session-kickoff/manifest-check.sh:285-311` requires the newest watch-touching commit to be
an ancestor of the newest re-stamp, so it fails at the build tip; the tracked pre-push hook runs the
full bar, so the landing push is blocked. Neither spec-10 §7 nor spec-6 §7 names the ratchet leg.

**The M6 collision across nodes.** The sibling spec on node c is OPEN, Tier-2, and its subject is
M6's parallelism inversion; its backlog row at `memory/backlog/TOOL.md:55` is OPEN. Spec-9 cites
neither. `git check-attr merge` reports `unspecified` for the build-method guide, so the row-keyed
driver that covers the decisions log and the backlog shards does not cover this pair.

**The byte-cap handoff.** `grep -c '25600\|curation-debt\|headroom'` over spec-10 returns 0. Spec-4
§8 assigns the decision to unit 5; spec-5 §5 and §8 assign it to unit 10; the README's parked items
do not carry it. The largest README measures 24715 bytes against the 25600 tier.

**Spec-1's AC2.** §4 Migration is a four-row table of counts; it names one filename, and not five.
Five READMEs carry non-blank content after the closing generated marker (aRuledFrontispiece 134
lines, aSiftedPlaybook 120, cTracedPromise 23, cKeyedLaunchpad 11, aTimedTurnstile 10), and the
sixth violator has its roster pair at lines 90 and 104 with the generated open at 206 — a
plan-before-prose violation S4's single stated trigger does not fire on.

## What the lenses REFUTED

cleared.

  distinct names, no collisions, no spec spelling another's pair differently.
- **Verb-spelling drift.** One spelling per verb across specs 1, 6 and 10; the mode tuple and usage
  line spec-6 cites are exactly where it says.
- **Three names for one refusal.** Spec-1 AC4, spec-2 AC7 and spec-3 AC5 all resolve to the single
  unequal-marker-count message; `apply_region` carries exactly the three refusals spec-1 S3 claims.

  edit" holds.

  21 nested, 1 non-markdown, 1 build with a prompts folder, 5 headerless specs, 5 grandfathered

- **Spec-8's five-fact inventory.** The worktree read, both terminal run-state files, the terminal

## What needs an OWNER turn rather than a spec edit

- **The roster pair's contents, at corpus scale.** Fork 1 resolved Option A for this build's own
  README. Applying it to twelve existing rosters means moving or dropping live state columns in
  other builds' authored tables — including one on another node whose own spec is open against the
  same question. A spec edit can restate fork 1; only the owner can authorise editing another
  build's roster to comply.
- **The byte tier for the largest README.** With the regions actually wired, the corpus's biggest
  entry point breaches the tier the owner set. The remedy is a curation pass, a grandfather row in
  an empty shrink-only registry, or a different number. All three are owner calls, and the parked
  items do not include this one.
- **Whether unit 6 or unit 10 lands last.** The README's ordering section is an owner-facing record
  that already re-sequenced this build once. Two specs now contradict it in opposite directions;
  which one gives way changes what "the retrofit commit is a pure re-render" means, and that rule is
  the README's, not a spec's.
- **The fifth parked item.** It was raised and never put to the owner. It is unaffected by anything
  above and stays open.
