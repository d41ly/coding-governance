## Needs an owner turn

**The byte tier.** The largest build README is 24715 bytes against a 25600-byte tier, and the three
new regions add roughly 2200. No unit may raise the number — the owner set it — and the repo's
existing mechanism for exactly this case is a row in the shrink-only `memory/project/curation-debt.txt`,
which is empty today. The build proceeds on that mechanism and the owner may overrule it; what a
spec may not do is quietly widen a number the owner chose.

**Restructuring another node's roster table.** Fork 1's Option A split, applied at corpus scale,
means moving derived columns out of roster tables in build records owned by nodes `b` and `c`. The
prior audit named this as an owner item and it was answered by a spec rather than by an owner. It is
recorded in the build README's Parked section as P6 rather than treated as settled.
