## Refutation patterns worth keeping

47 of 87 raw findings were refuted, and three causes account for most of them.

- **Reading a section in isolation.** A dozen findings die on a clause one or two sentences further
  on: the reuse audit that names the helper fixes the spelling a criterion appeared to forbid; the
  Migration paragraph's second half makes its first half unambiguous; §5's refusal list arms a
  vacuity a separate finding calls unarmed. These specs single-source aggressively and reward being
  read whole.
- **Treating an open question as a contradiction.** Five findings ask a spec to close a fork in §8
  before the build. Forks are deliberately open here, and the method resolves them at the pass that
  builds the unit; a scope item its own §8 questions is the normal pre-build state.

  it stands, observe that it does not cover the new thing, and file the criterion as false — when
  the criterion is the instruction to extend it. Unit 2's L1 is the one case where the wording is
  genuinely ambiguous enough that both readings are defensible.

The inverse pattern is what produced most of the confirmed set: a criterion citing a real gate that
runs on the real bar, where the gate's population or predicate cannot see the thing claimed. Six of
nine highs. Every one of them was found by running the gate, not by reading it.
