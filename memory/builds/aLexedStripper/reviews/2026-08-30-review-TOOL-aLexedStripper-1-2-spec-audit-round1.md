Two confirmed blockers, both in `TOOL-aLexedStripper-2`, and both in the same direction: the unit whose own §5 says "a fail-open regression is the whole risk" ships two measured fail-open regressions on the only mechanical control against unbounded agent fan-out, and neither is observable by any criterion in its §6. None of those stops the unit from being built; all four stop it from being audited by anyone but its author.

Neither spec's author appears to have read the other's §5. That is the strongest argument for gating the pair together rather than per unit.
