## Claim 1 — a fully derived roster destroys the planned-but-unspecced signal

**Verdict:** PARTIAL · confidence high

### The claim, corrected so it is true

Deriving the build roster purely from spec status headers destroys a signal that only the AUTHORED marker pair can carry, because a derived roster cannot name a unit that has no spec yet. The unattended kit reads the authored `roster:units` pair on purpose and at exactly one site: `roster_ids` at tools/unattended/unattended.sh:1487 (comment at :1476-1486 records that pointing it at the generated region was TRIED and made the comparison a tautology). It feeds `missing_units` at :1512, which has two callers — `verb_plan` at :1614 and `build-complete` Definition-of-Done term 3 at :2717. Term 3 IS overridable: `build-complete` sits in DOD_CORE (:249) but not in DOD_NO_OVERRIDE (:270), so `--override build-complete --reason <text>` is accepted and `is_overridden` skips the check outright (:2352); the driver's own comments at :2682 and :2691 spell that exit. On the 50 READMEs with no pair the term passes VACUOUSLY (:1490, :1513), and on the 11 that carry one the signal has never fired — measured 0 missing on all of them, with `aStandingWrit`'s pair inert because it wraps S0..S8 handles rather than ids. There is no alternative carrier: front-matter `ids:` is derived by `rosters()` from a read set that skips a build's own README for its own slug (tools/memory-tree/gen_build_index.py:535-537).

### What the build must do differently

Do NOT point `roster_ids` at the generated `gen:build-units` region. That exact change was made and reverted inside TOOL-aBoundedVerdict-11: the generated region is rendered from the specs that exist, so `roster_ids` becomes a subset of `spec_ids` by construction and `missing_units` is empty always (unattended.sh:1477-1481, the repo's own assertion-between-two-derived-values class). If the new README contract derives the roster, keep a separate authored carrier for planned-but-unspecced units or `--plan`'s MISSING state and build-complete term 3 both become dead code. Two facts should down-rank this from BLOCKING to a design constraint rather than a stop-ship: term 3 is bought with `--override build-complete` by the run itself, and the signal is currently vacuous on 51 of 61 builds and reports zero on the other 10. Independently, the map dossier memory/map/features/build-readme-surface.md must be corrected at :55 and :99-101 before anyone designs against it — it is the source that told two finders the pair was retired.

### Fork for the owner

The authored pair is live on 10 of 61 builds and has never once reported a missing unit, yet it is the only structure in the kit that can express a planned-but-unspecced unit. Ruling needed: make the pair MANDATORY and gate its presence (turning term 3 into a real check), or accept the signal's loss and delete `roster_ids`, `missing_units` and build-complete term 3 rather than shipping a term that passes vacuously on 84% of the corpus.
