## Verdict: BLOCKED

Four blockers stand, and the tally sits here rather than on the verdict line because that line's token is a closed set. Three of the four are scope omissions of the same shape: a coupled artifact that the unit's own prior art already names is missing from the files-touched list, so the criteria pass over an artifact left wrong. Two of them are already wrong IN THE TREE at the time of writing — `tools/unattended/.unattended.conf.example` still declares a floor of 15 against a 16-member core, and `tools/memory-tree/BUILD-METHOD.template.md` is 8 bytes over a budget this run is forbidden to raise. The fourth is a mis-identified subject: unit 6 grades and announces a change to "checks 22 and 23" while the block it edits holds check 23 alone.

Every one of the four is cheap to fix — a value, eight bytes of trim, a retitle plus one comment correction, and a scope line. None needs a design change and none is a fork. The verdict is BLOCKED on the ACs, not on the ideas: the goal each unit states is sound and the mechanisms chosen are the right ones.
