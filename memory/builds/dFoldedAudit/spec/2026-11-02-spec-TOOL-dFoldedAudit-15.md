### S2 — the floor

`b`, `h`, `m`, `f` and `p` are the row's blockers, highs, minors, folded and promoted. A row names no
severity per fold, so the floor is the least number of units any honest split of `f` can build. M4
gives each promoted blocker and high its own unit and the promoted minors one. Folds that fall on
blockers and highs lower the first term, and they spend the fold that could have left no minor. That
least is:

```
owe = b + h > f ? b + h - f + (m > 0 ? 1 : 0) : 1
```

It holds only where `p > 0`. A row with `p = 0` is a fold and owes nothing, as today. F1 records why
this is neither of the two corrections the report carries, and §4 is written to its resolution.

| Site | Today | After |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/unattended/unattended.sh:12987-12990`, in `verb_review` | `max(1, p - m)` | the expression above, in bash |
| `tools/unattended/check-unattended.sh:893`, `:903`, `:911` | each stores `pp[it] = pr` | each stores `fg[it] = fo`; `pp` has no other reader |
| `tools/unattended/check-unattended.sh:930-931` | `owe = pp[it] - mm[it]` | the expression over `bl`, `hh`, `mm` and `fg` |

A row short of its counts takes `owe = 1`, as it does today by arithmetic: `hc[it]` is false or
`fg[it]` is negative, and `specbad` or `rowbad` reds that row anyway. The comments at
`tools/unattended/unattended.sh:12961-12965` and `tools/unattended/check-unattended.sh:922-926` state the
new floor. The note at `:13012` now tells the truth under `counted`, because `f` is 0 there, and it
keeps its bytes.

Suite moves: `FR_PROM`'s expected floor and `tools/unattended/unattended.test.sh:2536` become 3, and
`FR_PROM`'s green control moves from `D_THREE` to `D_FOUR`. Unit 2 is CLOSED, so its AC10 stays as
